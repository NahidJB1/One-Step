Cost of Living in Malaysia for International Students 2026

Complete 2026 breakdown of the cost of living in Malaysia for international students. Monthly expenses for accommodation, food, transport, and tuition fees explained.

One of the main reasons Malaysia is a top choice for international students is its high standard of living combined with very affordable costs. Compared to the US, UK, or Australia, you can live comfortably in Malaysia for a fraction of the price.

1. Monthly Expenses Breakdown

Expense Category Estimated Monthly Cost (RM) Notes
Accommodation RM 400 - RM 1,500 Depends on whether you stay in a shared campus hostel or rent a private apartment.
Food & Dining RM 400 - RM 700 Eating at campus cafeterias and local 'Mamak' stalls is very cheap.
Transportation RM 100 - RM 200 Public transport (LRT, MRT, buses) is heavily subsidized for students.
Phone & Internet RM 50 - RM 100 Malaysia has excellent, cheap 4G/5G mobile plans.
Books & Supplies RM 100 - RM 200 Can be lower if buying used books or using digital resources.
Total Estimated Cost RM 1,100 - RM 2,700 Roughly $240 to $600 USD per month.

2. Accommodation: On-Campus vs Off-Campus

On-Campus Hostels (RM 400 - RM 800/month): The most affordable and convenient option for first-year students. Universities like MMU, UPM, and KLUST offer great on-campus housing with utilities usually included.

Off-Campus Apartments (RM 700 - RM 1,500/month): Many students prefer renting a condominium near the campus with a swimming pool and gym. Sharing a 3-bedroom condo with friends can bring the cost down to RM 500 per person.

3. City Comparison: Where is it cheapest?

Kuala Lumpur (KL) & Subang Jaya: More expensive. Expect to pay at the higher end of the scale (RM 2,000+ per month) due to higher rent and entertainment costs. (e.g., Taylor's University, APU)

Cyberjaya & Kajang: Very affordable student cities. Rent is much lower, and the areas are designed for student living. Expect around RM 1,200 - RM 1,500 per month. (e.g., MMU, University of Cyberjaya, KLUST)

Outside KL (Perak, Melaka, Penang): The most affordable. Cost of living can be as low as RM 1,000 per month.
