Malaysia Student Visa Requirements: Document Checklist

Use this checklist before sending your documents to the university. It focuses on a new Student Pass application for higher education; your nationality and application category can add requirements.

By Nahid Jahan Bhuiyan · Reviewed 21 September 2026 · 4 min read

The quick answer

Prepare your passport copy, compliant photo, offer, academic records, English evidence and health declaration. Check the full EMGS instructions and your institution’s current checklist before submission.

The core document checklist

New Student Pass documents: check against the current EMGS requirements

Photograph35 × 45 mm with a white background; follow EMGS’s full photo rules.

Academic recordsSupply the certificates and transcripts required for your admission and application.

Conditional documentsCountry-specific documents, previous-pass evidence and institutional requests depend on your circumstances.

Checklist source: EMGS: required documents. That source recommends passport validity extending 12 months beyond the course length for a one-off pass application. Follow the full country-specific instructions rather than treating this summary as an exhaustive list.

A pre-submission quality check

  • Identity matches: compare your name, birth date and passport number across the offer and application. Ask admissions how to resolve differences before submitting.
  • Scans are readable: keep page edges visible, avoid glare and check every page at normal zoom. Follow the portal’s current size and file-type limits.
  • The offer is the right version: check the course, campus and intake. An old offer may need to be replaced after deferral.
  • Documents are complete: use the institution’s requirements for certification and translation. Do not edit an official document to make details match.
  • English conditions are explicit: ask which test, score and evidence your course and application require. Do not assume one university’s waiver applies to another.
  • You have a record: retain copies, payment receipts and the application reference. Use official submission channels for identity documents.

What changes after approval and arrival?

Document preparation does not end with submission. Keep your current passport, offer, approval and any required entry documents available for the journey. Ask the university for its arrival checklist and the procedure for handing over your passport for endorsement.

Medical screening deadline

EMGS’s medical-screening guidance gives new international students 7 working days after arrival to attend a panel clinic. Arrange this with your international office promptly; screening is needed for the endorsement process.

Source: EMGS: medical screening. Tell your institution if you are transferring or progressing from another course, since those categories have different screening rules.

Questions to send your international office

Send a short message containing your nationality, intended programme, intake, passport expiry date and whether this is a new application, transfer or renewal. Ask:

  1. Which documents and certifications apply to my application category?
  2. Are there any additional country-specific requirements?
  3. Who will submit my application and how do I track it?
  4. What is the complete fee breakdown, insurance arrangement and refund policy?
  5. What are the latest submission date and deferral arrangements?
  6. Which documents and appointments must I prepare for arrival?

Bangladeshi applicants should ask for the checklist applicable to their nationality and chosen institution. Do not rely on a generic agent checklist as evidence that country-specific steps have been completed.

Questions students ask

Is IELTS compulsory for every programme?

Ask the institution which accepted evidence and score apply to your exact course and application. Do not assume either that IELTS is the only route or that an English requirement can be ignored.

Is the visa fee the same for every student?

No single amount is established by this checklist. Use the official fee calculator and request an itemised invoice for your application category, duration and related services.
