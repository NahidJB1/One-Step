Taylor’s page states that its fee schedule excludes 6% service tax for non-Malaysians on applicable charges, with exceptions for the international security deposit and EMGS application fees. Ask for the final invoice rather than applying tax indiscriminately to every item.

MMU’s programme-fee figure should not be treated as the complete cost of attendance. Its schedule also contains a payment structure and other charges. Ask the admissions office for an itemised total and payment dates. Neither example includes a personalised housing budget.

Compare what you will actually study

Make a module-by-module sheet for the two courses. For computing, look for programming, mathematics, algorithms, databases, systems, security and substantial assessed projects. The programme title alone does not reveal how much time you will spend in each area.

Taylor’s 2026 page lists Data Science, Cyber Security, Mobile Computing and Artificial Intelligence specialisations, and describes conventional, work-based learning and technopreneurship routes. Confirm availability and eligibility with the school. For your MMU offer, request the current programme handbook rather than assuming similarly named specialisations have identical modules.

  • Ask to see the final-year project brief and recent examples of assessed student work.
  • Confirm the internship duration, how placements are found and what support is available.
  • Check whether optional pathways change fees, location, duration or visa arrangements.
  • For credit transfer, obtain written exemptions and the remaining fee schedule before comparing totals.

Add the campus and living-cost decision

Get room quotes near the actual teaching location. Compare a realistic commute at class times, what utilities cost and whether the room contract continues during holidays. A course can fit your tuition budget but still create an uncomfortable monthly cash shortfall.

Plan the cash needed before classes: acceptance payment, visa-related charges, flights, housing deposit and initial living money. Keep deposits separate from ongoing expenses, and check when any scholarship is credited.

Questions students ask

Is Taylor’s always better because of an overall ranking?

An overall ranking does not establish which individual course, teaching arrangement or offer best fits you. Compare course-level evidence and your budget.

Can I subtract the two published fees to calculate my saving?

Not directly here. The figures use different currencies and may include different charges. Request comparable itemised invoices, then convert using a dated exchange rate if needed.
