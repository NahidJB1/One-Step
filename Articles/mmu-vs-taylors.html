The Verdict: Which should you choose?

Choose MMU if: You want to be a software engineer, data scientist, or IT professional. You prefer a quieter, tech-centric environment (Cyberjaya) and want a highly respected degree at a more affordable price point.

Choose Taylor's if: You want to study Business, Hospitality, or Creative Arts. You have a higher budget and want a premium, bustling campus lifestyle in the heart of Subang Jaya with unparalleled networking opportunities.
