Malaysia has become an incredibly generous education hub for international students. To attract top global talent, Malaysian private universities are offering massive tuition fee waivers and grants. Through One Step MY, you can easily apply to multiple scholarship funds.

Types of Scholarships Available

1. Academic Excellence (Merit-Based): These are awarded based on your high school (O-Level, A-Level, Matriculation) grades. The higher your grades, the higher your tuition fee waiver (up to 100%).

2. Talent & Sports Scholarships: If you are a national-level athlete or possess exceptional talent in the arts or e-sports, many universities (like Taylor's) will sponsor your education.

3. International Grant / Country Bursary: Some universities offer automatic partial scholarships just for being from a specific country (e.g., Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nigeria) to promote campus diversity.
