How to Study Medicine (MBBS) in Malaysia 2026

The complete guide for international students. Learn about entry requirements, fees, top medical universities, and the visa process.

Studying Medicine (MBBS) in Malaysia is increasingly popular among international students due to the high quality of education, globally recognized degrees, and significantly lower tuition fees compared to the US, UK, or Australia. Here is everything you need to know to get started in 2026.

1. Entry Requirements for MBBS

Medical degrees in Malaysia are highly regulated by the Malaysian Medical Council (MMC). Competition is tough, and the academic requirements are strict.

  • A-Levels: Minimum grades of BBB, ABC, or AAC in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics or Mathematics.
  • O-Levels / IGCSE: Minimum 5 Bs including Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Math, and English.
  • English Proficiency: IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL equivalent is mandatory for international students.
  • Interview: Almost all universities require a successful admissions interview (often conducted online for international students).

Important Note on MQA Accreditation

Always ensure the university you choose is accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognized by the medical council in your home country (e.g., BMDC for Bangladesh, NMC for India) so you can practice medicine when you return.

2. Program Duration & Structure

The standard MBBS program in Malaysia takes 5 years to complete.

  • Pre-Clinical (Years 1-2): Focused on foundational medical sciences (Anatomy, Biochemistry, Physiology) taught in campus labs and lecture halls.
  • Clinical (Years 3-5): Focused on practical, hands-on training. Students rotate through various departments (Surgery, Pediatrics, Internal Medicine) in government or private teaching hospitals.

4. Tuition Fees and Costs

Studying medicine is a significant investment. In Malaysia, the total tuition fee for a 5-year MBBS program ranges from RM 300,000 to RM 600,000 depending on the university. This is vastly cheaper than the $200,000+ USD it often costs in Western countries.
