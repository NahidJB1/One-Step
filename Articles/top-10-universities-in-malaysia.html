How to choose between two offers

  1. Check the actual qualification. Match the degree title, campus and delivery mode against MQA: Malaysian Qualifications Register. If a programme is absent, check MQA’s provisional accreditation portal and ask the institution to explain its status.
  2. Compare the syllabus. Look at compulsory modules, elective choices, assessment, placements and the final project. Ask for module outlines for the intake you will join.
  3. Compare the same costs. A total tuition figure and a first-year payment are not equivalent. Add mandatory non-tuition charges and a separate living budget.
  4. Check entry fit. Send your qualifications to admissions for an assessment. Do not assume a ranking tells you the required grades or English score.
  5. Test daily life. Ask about the commute, room availability, student support and access to facilities used by your course.

Questions students ask

Which university should I choose if my budget is limited?

Compare the complete written costs of the same course level and intake. A lower ranking does not establish a lower price, and a tuition discount may leave accommodation and other charges unchanged.

Does a QS rank prove that a degree is professionally recognised?

No. Check programme accreditation and any professional-body requirements for the country in which you want to work. University-wide rankings answer a different question.
