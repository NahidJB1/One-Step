Understand your BAC Education Group fee breakdown

Tuition and payment periods

The tables distinguish first-year tuition, following-year tuition and total tuition. Resource and visa-renewal charges appear in the notes and may recur. An overseas transfer stage needs its own university fee schedule and living budget.

Initial payments and additional charges

For a Malaysia-only route, read the tuition row alongside that institution's miscellaneous charges. For a transfer route, request separate Malaysia and overseas payment schedules, including any partner-university charges and currency.

Budgeting from Bangladesh

Use the currency and payment period printed in your programme's schedule. Keep accommodation, travel and living expenses separate from tuition. A BDT estimate needs a dated exchange rate and may differ from the amount your bank charges.

Compare scholarship or adjusted-fee columns only after your eligibility is confirmed. Your written offer should identify the award, covered charges and renewal conditions.

BAC Education Group fee chart: questions and answers

Are these BAC College fees or BAC Education Group fees?

The chart contains separate sections for BAC College and other BAC Education Group institutions. Match the institution and programme to your offer before using a tuition figure.

Does a BAC transfer programme fee include study overseas?

Do not assume a Malaysia tuition total includes the overseas stage. Check the programme's transfer arrangement, then obtain the partner university's tuition, compulsory charges and living-cost information separately.

What are BAC Education Group's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Where can I find the official BAC Education Group fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the BAC Education Group fees apply to?

The supplied schedule states: Valid from 1st January 2026 until 31st December 2026. Check individual course notes and confirm that the same rates apply to your intake.

Does BAC Education Group offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.
