Understand your INTI International University & Colleges fee breakdown

Tuition and payment periods

The main column is Total Tuition Fee (RM), not an annual or semester instalment. Tuition is not automatically the amount payable before arrival; additional application, registration, administration and deposit charges remain in the campus notes.

Initial payments and additional charges

Nilai, Subang and Penang schedules belong to different INTI institutions. Compare the exact programme, international-student category and fee period. Use the applicable source’s payment and tax notes; do not infer that a current official approximation has the same inclusions as a stored total.

Budgeting from Bangladesh

Use the currency and payment period printed in your programme's schedule. Keep accommodation, travel and living expenses separate from tuition. A BDT estimate needs a dated exchange rate and may differ from the amount your bank charges.

Compare scholarship or adjusted-fee columns only after your eligibility is confirmed. Your written offer should identify the award, covered charges and renewal conditions.

INTI fee chart: questions and answers

Do INTI Nilai, Subang and Penang use the same fees?

This chart has separate campus schedules. Compare the exact programme, qualification and campus; matching subject names do not establish identical tuition or additional charges.

Is the INTI tuition amount a yearly payment?

The tables label the amount as Total Tuition Fee (RM). Do not treat it as an annual or semester instalment. Use the campus notes and a written payment schedule for amounts due at each stage.

What are INTI's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Where can I find the official INTI fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the INTI fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does INTI offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.
