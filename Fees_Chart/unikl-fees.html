Understand your Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL) fee breakdown

Tuition and payment periods

Use the full fee-column label, including any one-year, semester or total basis. The campus, intake and duration columns describe the programme separately. Do not compare an annual foundation amount with a full-degree total as if they were equivalent.

Initial payments and additional charges

The stored notes distinguish a first payment from a second payment after visa approval. Security-bond and accommodation items can depend on nationality and campus; use the Bangladesh category and your selected institute's offer.

Budgeting from Bangladesh

Use the currency and payment period printed in your programme's schedule. Keep accommodation, travel and living expenses separate from tuition. A BDT estimate needs a dated exchange rate and may differ from the amount your bank charges.

Compare scholarship or adjusted-fee columns only after your eligibility is confirmed. Your written offer should identify the award, covered charges and renewal conditions.

UniKL fee chart: questions and answers

Which UniKL campus fee should I use?

Use the institute named in the CAMPUS column of your exact programme row and match it to your offer. UniKL's institution name does not mean every programme is taught at the same location.

Are UniKL fees annual or for the whole programme?

The fee basis is printed in each table heading. Some sections expressly identify a one-year amount. Retain that label and request a payment schedule if the period you need is not provided.

What are UniKL's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Where can I find the official UniKL fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the UniKL fees apply to?
