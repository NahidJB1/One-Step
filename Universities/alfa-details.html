About the University

Alfa University College (AUC) is a premier boutique institution known for its hands-on approach to creative arts and education. Originally established as a design academy, it has grown into a full-fledged university college that emphasizes close mentorship and industry-relevant skills.

It is particularly famous for its Early Childhood Education (ECE) program (linked with REAL Kids) and its award-winning School of Visual Communication.

The Boutique Campus

Located in USJ 1, Subang Jaya, the campus is designed to foster creativity. Unlike massive universities, Alfa offers a close-knit environment where lecturers know every student by name. The location is easily accessible and surrounded by student conveniences.
