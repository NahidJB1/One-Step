Frequently Asked Questions About ALFA University College

Where is ALFA University College in Malaysia?

ALFA's official programme website lists its address at 13, Persiaran Kewajipan, USJ 1, Subang Jaya, Selangor. Confirm your programme's teaching location before arranging accommodation.

How do I check an ALFA programme's accreditation?

Match the exact qualification title and programme reference with the Malaysian Qualifications Register or the relevant provisional accreditation record. An institution-level ranking does not replace programme accreditation.

What is the QS World ranking of ALFA University College in 2027?

ALFA University College has no institution entry in the published QS World University Rankings 2027 table.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

What is the QS Asia ranking of ALFA University College in 2026?

A QS Asia University Rankings 2026 position for ALFA University College has not been verified in our sources.

Sources: QS Asia University Rankings 2026 - Malaysia

What is the ranking of ALFA University College in Malaysia?

ALFA University College has no separate Malaysia position in the QS World 2027 table because it has no institution entry listed under Malaysia. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give ALFA University College an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

University perspective / ALFA

ALFA University College: look closely at how creative courses are taught

A Subang Jaya setting where studios, briefs and portfolio work matter as much as the programme title.

ALFA University College is based in Subang Jaya, Selangor. Its listed courses include design and built-environment subjects alongside business, education and postgraduate options. For a student choosing a creative field, the useful question is not simply whether the subject is available. It is how often students work in studios, which tools they use and what they are expected to produce by graduation.

The campus is an urban learning environment rather than a large residential estate. Images of design work, computer facilities, exhibition areas and the library help show the spaces students may use. Prospective applicants should still ask to see the facilities connected to their own course, because an interior design studio, an early-childhood learning room and a general computer lab serve very different needs.

Portfolio-based study can bring costs that do not appear in a tuition headline. Materials, printing, model making, specialist software and a suitable computer may all affect the real budget. Ask how projects are assessed, whether group work is common and which pieces can be developed into a professional portfolio. Students entering a built-environment field should also check any programme-specific recognition needed for later professional practice.

Before choosing an ALFA programme, confirm the exact award, study mode, intake and current accreditation record. The Malaysian Qualifications Register can be used to check an individual Malaysian qualification. Pair that check with the latest written fee schedule and a realistic plan for housing and travel in Subang Jaya.
