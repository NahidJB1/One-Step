University overview

About Asia Metropolitan University

Established in 2004, Asia Metropolitan University (AMU) has grown into a dynamic private university focused on delivering career-oriented education. Originally founded as the Masterskill School of Nursing and Healthcare, AMU achieved full university status in 2012 and expanded its academic portfolio into Business Management, Information Technology, Computer Science, and Allied Health Sciences.

Fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognized internationally by the Accreditation Service for International Schools, Colleges & Universities (ASIC), AMU provides industry-focused diplomas, bachelor's degrees, and postgraduate qualifications designed to equip graduates with practical skills for the modern workforce.

University perspective / AMU

Asia Metropolitan University: compare the practical side of each course

A career-oriented programme list where facilities, delivery and current recognition should be checked carefully.

Asia Metropolitan University offers programmes across business, information technology and health-related areas. The subjects differ substantially in how they are taught, so a broad university description is only the beginning. A student should compare modules, contact hours, assessment and the facilities connected to the exact qualification.

The campus gallery shows teaching rooms, shared spaces and the wider student environment. Applicants to computing should ask about lab access, software and project work. Those considering health-related study should check where practical training takes place, how placements are organised and whether equipment, uniforms or travel add to the listed fees.

Course recognition must be checked at qualification level. Use the Malaysian Qualifications Register for the specific programme and intake, and contact any relevant professional body if the goal is a regulated career. International recognition can vary by country, so a general statement about the university should not replace that programme-level check.

Before applying, request the latest course structure and written fee schedule. Confirm the teaching location, intake, duration and study mode on the offer letter. A realistic budget should include housing, transport and compulsory course expenses as well as tuition, particularly when practical sessions or placements occur away from the main campus.
