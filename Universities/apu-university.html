University overview

About Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation

Founded in 1993 originally as the Asia Pacific Institute of Information Technology (APIIT), Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) has grown into one of Malaysia's premier private universities. Located in Technology Park Malaysia (TPM), Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur, APU is renowned for offering technology-driven, industry-focused education that prepares graduates for high-demand digital global careers.

APU is ranked #=528 in QS World University Rankings 2027. APU holds a QS 5-Stars Plus rating. Recognized by MDEC as a Premier Digital Tech Institution, APU partners with industry leaders like Microsoft, AWS, Cisco, and IBM, and offers dual degrees with De Montfort University (DMU), UK.

Frequently Asked Questions About Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation APU

Which Asia Pacific University does this guide cover?

This guide covers Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. It does not cover other institutions using the Asia Pacific University name or APU abbreviation.

Are APU international merit awards automatic?

No automatic award is established by this guide. APU publishes international merit-award conditions and retains award discretion. Check your qualification, eligible programme and continuation requirements against the current official terms.

What is the QS World ranking of Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) in 2027?

APU is ranked #=528 in QS World University Rankings 2027.

Sources: Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) - QS profile · QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1)

Sources: APU 2026 brochure - QS Asia 2026 results

What is the ranking of Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) in Malaysia?

APU has Malaysia position #12 among the 32 institutions listed under Malaysia in QS World 2027, calculated from the published table. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Which year do the APU fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does APU offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.

Are APU degrees accredited?

APU is described as APU is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. The exact programme should be checked separately. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

When does APU's English programme start?

The listed information includes August, September, October and November 2023 dates and describes each level as four weeks. These dates are historical. Intakes and application deadlines can differ by programme and campus; confirm the current dates before arranging travel or a visa application.

When are APU's academic intakes?

The FAQ directs readers to the individual course page. Intakes and application deadlines can differ by programme and campus; confirm the current dates before arranging travel or a visa application.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.0 or TOEFL 500+ for foundation, IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL 513+ for bachelor's study, and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 550+ for postgraduate study. The TOEFL format is not clearly identified. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

APU is described as yes and quotes approximate monthly US-dollar ranges for single and limited family housing. The wording and prices appear incomplete and require confirmation. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

University perspective / APU

APU: choosing a technology degree by what you will actually build

Computing, engineering, design and digital business meet at a Bukit Jalil campus with many closely related course titles.

Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation is based in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur, and traces its roots to APIIT. Its programme list covers computing, cybersecurity, data, engineering, design and digital business among other areas. Closely related titles can contain very different modules, so applicants should compare the curriculum rather than choose from the specialisation name alone.

The campus has a contemporary technology-focused environment with teaching, laboratory and collaborative spaces. Ask how often students use specialist labs, what software and hardware are provided, and whether professional certification or equipment costs sit outside tuition. The strongest evidence of fit is often the work produced in modules, capstone projects and competitions.

Some APU routes involve an additional award connected with De Montfort University. Students considering that option should verify the exact award wording, eligibility and current terms for their programme. They should also distinguish an award arrangement from a physical transfer: any overseas study period, if offered, needs separate cost, credit and visa planning.

For an international applicant, compare the course structure, awarding body, intake and complete written fees together. Ask how internships are arranged and whether a placement is guaranteed, competitive or optional. The programme and fee data on this page can narrow the choices; the current offer letter should settle the final route and amount payable.
