Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions About BAC Education Group BAC

Who awards a BAC Education Group qualification?

It depends on the programme. The official group catalogue identifies different providers and partner awards, including transfer routes. Use the exact course title and awarding institution when comparing offers.

Is BAC a single university?

BAC Education Group includes several education institutions. This guide covers the group; the institution delivering your course and the body awarding your qualification must be identified separately.

What is the QS World ranking of BAC Education Group in 2027?

BAC Education Group has no institution entry in the published QS World University Rankings 2027 table.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

What is the QS Asia ranking of BAC Education Group in 2026?

A QS Asia University Rankings 2026 position for BAC Education Group has not been verified in our sources.

Sources: QS Asia University Rankings 2026 - Malaysia

What is the ranking of BAC Education Group in Malaysia?

BAC Education Group has no separate Malaysia position in the QS World 2027 table because it has no institution entry listed under Malaysia. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give BAC Education Group an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Are these BAC College fees or BAC Education Group fees?

The chart contains separate sections for BAC College and other BAC Education Group institutions. Match the institution and programme to your offer before using a tuition figure.

Does a BAC transfer programme fee include study overseas?

Do not assume a Malaysia tuition total includes the overseas stage. Check the programme's transfer arrangement, then obtain the partner university's tuition, compulsory charges and living-cost information separately.

What are BAC Education Group's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Where can I find the official BAC Education Group fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the BAC Education Group fees apply to?

The supplied schedule states: Valid from 1st January 2026 until 31st December 2026. Check individual course notes and confirm that the same rates apply to your intake.

Does BAC Education Group offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.

University perspective / BAC

BAC Education Group: start with the institution behind the course

Law may be the best-known route, but the BAC name covers several providers, awards and study settings.

BAC Education Group grew around Brickfields Asia College and now brings several education providers under one group. Law remains its most recognisable area, while the course list also reaches business, computing, media, hospitality and postgraduate study. That range is useful, but it makes the small print especially important: the BAC brand, the teaching institution and the body that awards a qualification are not always the same thing.

The group has an urban presence in Kuala Lumpur and Petaling Jaya. Libraries, moot court facilities, collaborative spaces and student events shape the environment shown in the gallery. A city-based campus may suit a student who values public transport and access to professional firms, although the daily journey, facilities and timetable depend on the institution and location named for the selected programme.

Students considering a partner or transfer route should map the whole degree before applying. Ask where every stage is taught, whether progression depends on grades, what appears on the final certificate and whether an overseas period changes the visa or total cost. For law, recognition and professional training requirements must also be checked in the country where the graduate intends to practise.

Use the course and fee section on this page as a shortlist, then request the latest programme outline and written fee schedule for the intended intake. Compare tuition with registration charges, learning materials, accommodation and transport. A careful programme-level comparison gives a much clearer picture than relying on a group-wide reputation or a partner university name alone.
