About the University

Binary University is one of Malaysia's longest-established private universities, renowned for its focus on **Management, IT, and Entrepreneurship**. It brands itself as a "Specialist University" that produces Industry Specialist Professionals (ISPs).

With a 5-Star rating for Employability, Binary is famous for its "ISP" ecosystem, where students are trained not just to be employees, but to think like industrial leaders and entrepreneurs.

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give Binary University an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.
