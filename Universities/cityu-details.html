About the University

City University Malaysia is a leading private university with a strong reputation for academic excellence and industry relevance. Located in the heart of Petaling Jaya, it offers a vibrant campus life and easy access to the city center.

With over 35 years of experience in producing employability-ready graduates, City U provides a wide range of programs from Foundation to PhD levels.
