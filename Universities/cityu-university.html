Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions About City University Malaysia

Is City University Malaysia public or private?

The QS institutional profile identifies City University Malaysia as a private institution in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia. Confirm the teaching campus for your selected programme separately.

Is CityU's QS Asia position a QS World ranking?

No. QS Asia and QS World are separate ranking tables. Use the publisher, edition and table label shown in the ranking section; a QS Stars rating is also a different assessment.

What is the QS World ranking of City University Malaysia in 2027?

City University Malaysia has no institution entry in the published QS World University Rankings 2027 table.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

What is the QS Asia ranking of City University Malaysia in 2026?

City University Malaysia is ranked #=558 in QS Asia University Rankings 2026.

Sources: City University Malaysia - QS profile

What is the ranking of City University Malaysia in Malaysia?

City University Malaysia has no separate Malaysia position in the QS World 2027 table because it has no institution entry listed under Malaysia. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give City University Malaysia an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Are City University degrees accredited?

CityU is described as the university is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Programme-level accreditation should be checked separately. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+ for undergraduate study and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+ for postgraduate study. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

CityU is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

Where is City University located?

The listed address is 8 Jalan 51A/223, Section 51A, 46100 Petaling Jaya, Selangor. Use the campus stated on the offer letter because teaching locations and available programmes can differ.

University perspective / CityU

City University Malaysia: the teaching location is part of the decision

A broad course list across several locations means applicants should match every programme to its campus.

City University Malaysia lists programmes in fields ranging from business and computing to design, education and health-related study. Its published campus information names Petaling Jaya, Cyberjaya and Johor Bahru, with Kota Kinabalu identified as a regional centre. The presence of a location does not establish that every course is taught there, so the programme and teaching site should always be checked together.

Petaling Jaya places students within the wider Klang Valley, while the other locations create different travel, housing and daily-cost decisions. Campus photographs provide useful context, but they cannot confirm which building or specialist room a faculty uses. Ask for the exact address, timetable pattern and facility information for the intended programme before choosing accommodation.

City University offers qualifications at several study levels. Compare modules, assessment, practical work and any internship or clinical component rather than relying on the subject title alone. A programme involving a lab, studio or placement may have different scheduling and additional costs from a lecture-based course, even when both appear under the same faculty.

For a reliable budget, read the written fee schedule beside the course duration and intake. Ask what is included, when each payment is due and whether materials, examinations or practical activities create separate charges. International applicants should also confirm the campus on the offer letter before beginning visa, travel or accommodation arrangements.
