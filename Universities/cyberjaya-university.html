University overview

About University of Cyberjaya

The University of Cyberjaya (UoC) is a top-tier healthcare university in Malaysia, rated 5-Stars by QS. Located in Malaysia's first smart city, it is renowned for its Medical, Pharmacy, and Psychology programs.

With a modern, green-friendly campus and strong partnerships with MERCY Malaysia, UoC focuses on producing holistic graduates with a passion to care and serve the community.

Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions About University of Cyberjaya

Where is the University of Cyberjaya?

The university lists its campus at Persiaran Bestari, Cyber 11, Cyberjaya, Selangor. Use the campus information and your programme's teaching arrangements when planning accommodation.

Do all University of Cyberjaya courses share medicine entry requirements?

No single medicine requirement should be applied to every course. Identify the exact programme and ask for its academic, subject and English requirements, including any professional conditions.

What is the QS World ranking of University of Cyberjaya in 2027?

University of Cyberjaya is ranked #951–1000 in QS World University Rankings 2027.

Sources: University of Cyberjaya - QS profile · QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1)

What is the QS Asia ranking of University of Cyberjaya in 2026?

University of Cyberjaya is ranked #434 in QS Asia University Rankings 2026.

Sources: University of Cyberjaya - 2026 prospectus

What is the ranking of University of Cyberjaya in Malaysia?

University of Cyberjaya has Malaysia position #24–25 among the 32 institutions listed under Malaysia in QS World 2027, calculated from the published table. The world-rank band does not establish a more precise national order. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give University of Cyberjaya an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Are University of Cyberjaya degrees accredited?

UoC is described as the university is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Its additional quality claim is unsourced in the FAQ. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+ for undergraduate study and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+ for postgraduate study. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

UoC is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

University perspective / UoC

University of Cyberjaya: planning for health sciences and professional study

Medicine and health-related teaching shape the campus, with recognition and placements to check course by course.

The University of Cyberjaya is strongly associated with medicine, pharmacy, psychology and other health sciences, while its list also includes programmes outside clinical fields. This creates two separate decisions for an applicant: whether the subject is a good academic fit and whether the exact qualification supports the professional route they want to follow.

The campus is in Cyberjaya, a planned city south of Kuala Lumpur. Laboratories, anatomy teaching spaces, the library and shared areas reflect the needs of science and healthcare students. A gallery can show the main learning environment, but applicants should ask where practical sessions, clinical placements or community work take place because these may involve locations beyond the main campus.

Recognition is programme and jurisdiction specific. A medical, pharmacy or allied-health applicant should check the Malaysian accreditation record and contact the regulator in the country where they hope to practise. Entry requirements, clinical hours and licensing steps may be stricter than the general university admission requirements shown for other courses.

The full cost may include more than tuition. Ask about equipment, uniforms, insurance, health checks, placement travel and any examination charges relevant to the course. Confirm the intake and fee schedule in writing, then plan accommodation around the actual class and placement locations rather than the university name alone.
