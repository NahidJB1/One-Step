About the College

Kolej Teknologi Antarabangsa Cybernetics (KTAC) is a pioneer in technical and vocational education (TVET) in Malaysia. For over 25 years, it has produced skilled graduates ready for the workforce.

KTAC is well-regarded for its practical approach in Aviation Management, Hospitality, and Early Childhood Education. Its affordable fees and strategic location make it a top choice for students seeking career-focused qualifications.
