About the University

Genovasi University College is Malaysia's first and only design-thinking dedicated university. It is part of the global network of **d.schools** (alongside Stanford University and Hasso Plattner Institute, Germany).

Moving beyond traditional lectures, Genovasi integrates **Design Thinking** into every program, from Business to Media Arts, producing graduates who are innovative problem solvers ready for the future economy.

The Innovation Hub

Located in Section 13, Petaling Jaya, the campus is designed as an open, fluid space to encourage creativity. It sits in a strategic commercial hub, surrounded by businesses and accessible via public transport.
