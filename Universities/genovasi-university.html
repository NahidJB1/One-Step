University overview

About Genovasi University College

Genovasi University College is Malaysia's first and only design-thinking dedicated university. It is part of the global network of **d.schools** (alongside Stanford University and Hasso Plattner Institute, Germany).

Moving beyond traditional lectures, Genovasi integrates **Design Thinking** into every program, from Business to Media Arts, producing graduates who are innovative problem solvers ready for the future economy.

University perspective / Genovasi

Genovasi University College: understand how design thinking enters the course

A smaller programme list built around problem solving, business, media and postgraduate study.

Genovasi University College presents design thinking as a central part of its learning approach. Its current course list on this site includes business, accounting, media technology, education and doctoral study. The practical question for applicants is how that approach appears inside their chosen programme: through projects, workshops, client briefs, research or assessment.

A smaller institution can offer a more focused environment, but students should still examine the resources attached to the course. Ask about class format, access to software and production tools, supervision for major projects and the type of work previous students have completed. For media or design-related study, a portfolio of actual student outcomes is often more informative than a broad claim about creativity.

Postgraduate applicants need a different comparison from foundation or degree students. Research supervision, proposal requirements, delivery mode and expected contact with supervisors should be confirmed before applying. Business and education applicants should also check whether the programme is designed for working professionals and whether any weekend or blended schedule is available to international students.

Use the listed programmes and fees as an initial guide, then request the latest course structure, intake and full payment schedule. Confirm the exact qualification in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and ask what appears on the final award. Accommodation and transport should be planned around the current teaching address stated by the institution.
