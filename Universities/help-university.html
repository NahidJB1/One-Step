University overview

About HELP University

Founded in 1986, HELP University (Higher Education Learning Philosophy) has established itself as one of Malaysia's leading private higher education institutions. Renowned globally for academic excellence and industry relevance, HELP is a pioneer in credit transfer and twinning degree pathways with premier universities across the UK, Australia, the United States, and Canada.

Recognized as a Premier Digital Tech Institution (PDTI) by MDEC and awarded a 5-Star Rating by QS Stars, HELP University is famous for producing highly employable, industry-ready graduates. It hosts Southeast Asia's premier center for Psychology education alongside renowned faculties in Business, Law, Information Technology, Education, and Communication.

What is the ranking of HELP University in Malaysia?

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give HELP University an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

What are HELP University's tuition fees for international students?

Find your program and campus in the fee chart on this page, then read the relevant student category, fee columns and remarks. The schedule uses labels including Total Tuition Fees. Different programs and fee periods cannot be combined into one reliable tuition price. Amounts not shown need an intake-specific quotation.

What are HELP University's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Does the HELP University fee chart show fees per semester or per year?

Use the exact period printed in each column. A first-year payment, fee per semester and full-program total describe different costs. Where a semester or annual breakdown is absent, request a payment schedule; dividing a program total can give the wrong instalment amount.

Where can I find the official HELP University fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the HELP University fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does HELP University offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.

Are HELP degrees accredited?

HELP is described as HELP is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Individual programme records should be checked separately. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.0–5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+ for undergraduate study and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+ for postgraduate study. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

HELP is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

University perspective / HELP

HELP University: compare the campus and pathway behind the programme

Psychology, business and transfer options are prominent, but the exact award and teaching site still matter.

HELP University has a long association with psychology, business and international education pathways, while its course list extends into law, communication, education and technology. Students should begin with the exact programme structure. A local degree, a credit-transfer route and a partner programme can lead to different awards, locations and total costs even when they sit within the same subject area.

The university operates across more than one learning setting in the Klang Valley. That makes the campus on the offer letter important for accommodation and travel planning. Photos show parts of the campus environment and student spaces, but applicants should ask where their faculty teaches and whether the full programme remains at one site.

Psychology applicants should distinguish an undergraduate academic degree from the later professional training required for particular careers. Students considering transfer should map the credit requirements, progression rules, overseas tuition and visa costs before treating the pathway as a single package. For every subject, compare modules, assessment and practical experience rather than relying on the partner name.

Request the current intake, entry criteria and full fee schedule in writing. Ask what is included, when a transfer decision must be made and whether scholarships apply to every stage. The qualification's Malaysian accreditation record and the final offer letter provide the clearest basis for an application decision.
