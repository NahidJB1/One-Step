University overview

About International College of Management and Sports

ICMS (International College of Management and Sports, formerly known as International College of Music and Cyberlynx International College) is a premier private higher education institution in Malaysia. Founded with a vision to provide specialized, industry-focused qualifications, ICMS has evolved to offer cutting-edge programs in Business Management, Hospitality, Sports Science, Information Technology, and Law Enforcement.

With accreditation from the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and approval from the Ministry of Higher Education, ICMS blends practical skill development with academic rigor. The college fosters a vibrant learning environment, equipping domestic and international students with leadership capabilities, professional competencies, and real-world experience needed to excel in global markets.

What is the ranking of ICMS in Malaysia?

ICMS has no separate Malaysia position in the QS World 2027 table because it has no institution entry listed under Malaysia. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give ICMS an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

University perspective / ICMS

ICMS: a compact course list with an applied focus

Management, sports and professional subjects should be compared through facilities, practical work and the exact award.

ICMS is presented on this site as the International College of Management and Sports. Its programme list is more compact than those of large universities, with options connected to management, sports and other career-oriented fields. That can make comparison easier, provided the applicant checks the current institution name and exact qualification on official application documents.

The gallery shows a smaller campus environment with classrooms, student areas and library space. Applicants should ask which specialist facilities support their course. Sports-related study may require practical venues, fitness or performance equipment, while management programmes may rely more on projects, presentations and industry exposure.

Applied learning claims need concrete details. Ask how many practical hours are built into the programme, whether internships are assessed, who arranges placements and what happens if a preferred placement is unavailable. International students should also confirm that any work-based activity complies with the conditions attached to their student pass.

Before applying, verify the award title, accreditation record, intake, duration and full fee schedule. Ask about compulsory equipment, uniforms, transport or placement costs. Use the current offer letter as the source for payment and visa planning, especially if older pages or documents use a different expansion of the ICMS name.
