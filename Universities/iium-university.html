University overview

About International Islamic University Malaysia

Established in 2004, International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) has grown into a dynamic private university focused on delivering career-oriented education. Originally founded as the Masterskill School of Nursing and Healthcare, IIUM achieved full university status in 2012 and expanded its academic portfolio into Business Management, Information Technology, Computer Science, and Allied Health Sciences.

Fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognized internationally by the Accreditation Service for International Schools, Colleges & Universities (ASIC), IIUM provides industry-focused diplomas, bachelor's degrees, and postgraduate qualifications designed to equip graduates with practical skills for the modern workforce.

Frequently Asked Questions About International Islamic University Malaysia

What is the QS World ranking of International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) in 2027?

IIUM is ranked #=691 in QS World University Rankings 2027.

Sources: International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) - QS profile · QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1)

What is the QS Asia ranking of International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) in 2026?

IIUM is ranked #=142 in QS Asia University Rankings 2026.

Sources: QS Asia University Rankings 2026 - Malaysia

What is the ranking of International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) in Malaysia?

IIUM has Malaysia position #17 among the 32 institutions listed under Malaysia in QS World 2027, calculated from the published table. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

University perspective / IIUM

IIUM: an international university shaped by Islamic values

A public university with several campuses, a wide academic range and a distinctive campus culture.

International Islamic University Malaysia is a public university commonly known as IIUM. It combines a broad academic portfolio with an educational environment informed by Islamic values. The programme list includes undergraduate and postgraduate study across fields such as law, economics, engineering, information technology, architecture, humanities and health sciences.

IIUM operates across several campuses, so applicants should match the programme to its teaching location. Gombak is the best-known main campus, while health and other specialist study may be connected with different sites. The location affects accommodation, transport and daily routines, and the campus stated on the offer letter should guide those plans.

The university's international character can appeal to students seeking a multicultural Muslim learning environment. Applicants should still examine the curriculum, language requirements and faculty expectations for the exact course. Some programmes may use English, Arabic or Malay in different academic contexts, so language preparation should be discussed with the university rather than assumed.

As with any public-university application, programme availability, international entry routes and fees can vary by intake. Confirm the application channel, qualification requirements and payment schedule in writing. For regulated subjects, check programme-specific recognition and any practical or clinical obligations before accepting an offer.
