About the University

INTI International University & Colleges is one of Malaysia's most respected education groups. Known for its "International" focus, INTI offers the widest range of pathways to graduate with prestigious degrees from the UK, USA, Australia, and New Zealand.

The flagship campus in Nilai is a sprawling 82-acre township university, offering a complete campus lifestyle with on-site accommodation, sports facilities, and international diversity.
