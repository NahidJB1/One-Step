Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions About INTI International University

Are INTI Nilai and INTI Subang the same campus?

No. Nilai and Subang are separate study locations. The INTI course finder identifies the institution offering each programme; confirm this before comparing accommodation, intakes or awards.

Does INTI's university ranking apply to every college programme?

The ranking record identifies INTI International University. A university ranking does not establish a separate ranking for every INTI college, partner award or individual programme.

What is the QS World ranking of INTI International University in 2027?

INTI is ranked #=406 in QS World University Rankings 2027.

Sources: INTI International University - QS profile · QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1)

What is the QS Asia ranking of INTI International University in 2026?

INTI is ranked #=122 in QS Asia University Rankings 2026.

Sources: INTI International University - QS profile

What is the ranking of INTI International University in Malaysia?

INTI has Malaysia position #10 among the 32 institutions listed under Malaysia in QS World 2027, calculated from the published table. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give INTI International University an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Are INTI degrees accredited?

INTI is described as INTI is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. The exact institution and programme should be checked separately. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

What English score is required?

For undergraduate study it lists TOEFL iBT 42, IELTS 5.0, MUET Band 3, PTE Academic 47, CEFR B1 or CEPT 154. For postgraduate study it lists TOEFL iBT 60, IELTS 6.0, MUET Band 4, PTE Academic 59, CEFR B2 or CEPT 169. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

INTI is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

Where is INTI located?

The FAQ lists Nilai, Subang Jaya, Penang and Sabah. Applicants should confirm which named INTI institution teaches their programme. Use the campus stated on the offer letter because teaching locations and available programmes can differ.

University perspective / INTI

INTI International University: choose the campus, course and award together

Nilai, Subang and Penang create different study experiences within the wider INTI network.

The INTI name covers more than one institution and campus. INTI International University is in Nilai, while the Subang and Penang locations include separately named INTI colleges. This distinction matters because similar subjects can lead to different qualifications, partners or teaching locations. A student should start with the full programme listing rather than assume every INTI course belongs to the university in Nilai.

Nilai offers a larger campus setting with residence, sports and study facilities. Subang and Penang feel more urban, which changes daily questions about commuting, nearby housing and access to the city. The images on this page show parts of the wider INTI environment, but the campus on the offer letter is the one that should guide accommodation and travel plans.

INTI lists routes across business, computing, engineering and other fields, including transfer and partner arrangements. Those options can be valuable for students seeking an international pathway, yet they need a stage-by-stage comparison. Check the awarding body, transfer conditions, overseas tuition, credit recognition and whether completing the full programme in Malaysia is possible.

For an international applicant, the written offer should settle the institution, campus, intake, duration and amount payable. Use the course cards and fee table here to identify options, then confirm the current schedule directly before paying. If a programme requires labs, placements or professional recognition, ask where those activities occur and whether they create extra travel or equipment costs.
