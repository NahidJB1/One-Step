University overview

About Kings University College

King's University College (formerly Kolej Bandar) is a premier city campus located in the heart of Kuala Lumpur. It is renowned for its **"Work-Ready Philosophy"**, ensuring graduates are not just academically qualified but industry-prepared.

Partnering with global institutions and industry leaders, King's offers a unique **"Earn as you Learn"** program, allowing students to gain paid work experience while studying, particularly in Hospitality and Business.

University perspective / Kings

Kings University College: what a city-campus course should show you

Central Kuala Lumpur brings access and energy, while work-based claims need programme-level detail.

Kings University College offers a city-campus experience in Kuala Lumpur, with listed programmes in areas such as business, computing and hospitality. The location can make access to transport, employers and city services convenient. It also makes housing and commuting an important part of the study decision, especially for students comparing the campus with institutions that have larger residential grounds.

The college promotes a work-ready approach and practical learning. Applicants should ask how that language translates into the timetable: whether there are assessed projects, employer briefs, internships or optional work experiences. If an opportunity is described as paid or connected to employment, request the current written terms and confirm how Malaysia's student-pass rules apply to international students.

A useful course comparison includes the awarding institution, programme recognition, class location and complete structure. Hospitality students may need to understand practical training and placement expectations, while computing students should ask about software, labs and project work. Business applicants can compare the balance between lectures, case studies and applied assignments.

Before paying, request a current offer that states the intake, duration, tuition and other compulsory charges. Build a monthly budget for rent, food and transport in Kuala Lumpur, then compare it with the actual timetable. A city location can be a strong advantage when the programme and practical opportunities are clearly documented.
