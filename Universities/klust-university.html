University overview

About KL University of Science & Technology

KLUST (formerly IUKL) is Malaysia's first and only university specializing in Infrastructure. For over 20 years, it has been the premier destination for Engineering, Architecture, and Quantity Surveying education.

Now rebranded as KLUST, it continues its legacy of producing "Technopreneurs" with a strong focus on practical skills, sustainability, and real-world technology application.

What are KLUST (IUKL)'s tuition fees for international students?

Find your program and campus in the fee chart on this page, then read the relevant student category, fee columns and remarks. The schedule uses labels including Total Fees. Different programs and fee periods cannot be combined into one reliable tuition price. Amounts not shown need an intake-specific quotation.

What are KLUST (IUKL)'s tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Does the KLUST (IUKL) fee chart show fees per semester or per year?

Use the exact period printed in each column. A first-year payment, fee per semester and full-program total describe different costs. Where a semester or annual breakdown is absent, request a payment schedule; dividing a program total can give the wrong instalment amount.

Where can I find the official KLUST (IUKL) fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. Which year do the KLUST (IUKL) fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does KLUST (IUKL) offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.

Are KLUST degrees accredited?

KLUST is described as the university is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Individual programme records should be checked separately. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

When are KLUST's intakes?

The listed information includes March, June and October or November for foundation, undergraduate and postgraduate study. Intakes and application deadlines can differ by programme and campus; confirm the current dates before arranging travel or a visa application.

What English score is required?

For undergraduate study it lists TOEFL iBT 42, IELTS 5.0, MUET Band 3, PTE Academic 47, CEFR B1 or CEPT 154. For postgraduate study it lists TOEFL iBT 60, IELTS 6.0, MUET Band 4, PTE Academic 59, CEFR B2 or CEPT 169. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

KLUST is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

Where is KLUST located?

Unipark Suria, Jalan Ikram–Uniten, 43000 Kajang, Selangor. Use the campus stated on the offer letter because teaching locations and available programmes can differ.

University perspective / KLUST

KLUST: an infrastructure-focused university with practical questions to ask

Engineering, architecture and the built environment sit at the centre of a campus outside central Kuala Lumpur.

Kuala Lumpur University of Science & Technology is the current name of the institution previously known as Infrastructure University Kuala Lumpur. Its course list is closely associated with engineering, architecture, quantity surveying, construction and technology, alongside business and other study routes. Students may still encounter the former IUKL name in older documents, so the current institution and award name should be checked on every application record.

The campus setting gives built-environment students a useful context for thinking about design, materials, infrastructure and sustainability. Laboratories, architecture spaces, the library and nearby accommodation appear in the page gallery. Facilities should be compared at faculty level, especially for courses that depend on surveying equipment, workshops, technical software or supervised practical work.

Professional pathways need more than a broad university description. An architecture, engineering or quantity-surveying applicant should check the precise qualification, accreditation status and any later registration steps relevant to the country where they plan to work. Ask how much of the course is project based, whether industrial training is required and how placements are arranged for international students.

KLUST is not in the centre of Kuala Lumpur, so transport and housing deserve their own budget. Compare the timetable with the location of any residence, food options and the likely commute. The fee table provides a starting point; the final decision should use a current offer letter that states the intake, duration, charges and payment schedule.
