About Limkokwing University

Founded in 1991 by the late Tan Sri Dato' Sri Paduka Dr. Lim Kok Wing, Limkokwing University of Creative Technology (LUCT) is an internationally recognized pioneer in creative, innovative, and industry-driven education. Headquartered in Cyberjaya, Malaysia's technological heartland, the university has established global learning hubs across Asia, Africa, and Europe.

Home to a vibrant international community of students from over 150 nations, the campus offers an unmatched environment where art, multimedia, technology, and global business converge.

Campus & Facilities

The flagship Cyberjaya campus is a futuristic, highly creative environment specifically built to simulate modern corporate creative production hubs. Featuring iconic jet-black decor, multi-camera television studios, and interactive student plazas, the campus serves as an incubator for creative talent.

Students enjoy a well-rounded lifestyle with access to university-branded retail outlets, sound production suites, a dedicated campus gym and fitness center, and vibrant global cultural exchange events.

  • Design & Fashion Atelier Labs with mannequin suites and fabric workstations
  • Multi-Camera Television & Film Production Studios with green screen technology
  • Sound & Music Design Recording Academy featuring acoustic control suites
  • On-Campus Gym & Fitness Center equipped with cardio and strength training gear
  • High-End Apple Mac & Animation Render Farms for 3D modeling and VFX
  • The Plaza, Wings Lifestyle Outlets, and International Food Court
  • Outdoor Recreational Swimming Pool and Sports Complex
  • Digital Resource Centre & E-Library with access to global academic databases
