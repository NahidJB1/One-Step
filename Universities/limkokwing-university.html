About Limkokwing University of Creative Technology

Founded in 1991 by the late Tan Sri Dato' Sri Paduka Dr. Lim Kok Wing, Limkokwing University of Creative Technology (LUCT) is an internationally recognized pioneer in creative, innovative, and industry-driven education. Headquartered in Cyberjaya, Malaysia's technological heartland, the university has established global learning hubs across Asia, Africa, and Europe.

Limkokwing University is world-famous for its distinctive jet-black architectural aesthetic, avant-garde design philosophy, and its revolutionary "Indusity" learning modelâ€”which directly integrates real industry projects into academic coursework. Home to a vibrant international community of students from over 150 nations, the campus offers an unmatched environment where art, multimedia, technology, and global business converge.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

It says foundation study does not require IELTS or TOEFL; bachelor's study generally requires IELTS 5.0–5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+; postgraduate study generally requires IELTS 5.5–6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+. Which registration documents are requested?

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

University perspective / Limkokwing

Limkokwing University: judge a creative course by the work it enables

Cyberjaya provides the setting; studios, briefs, feedback and student output reveal the programme.

Limkokwing University of Creative Technology is based in Cyberjaya and is associated with design, media and creative technology. Its listed subjects include graphic design, animation, fashion, film and digital production. Creative labels can sound similar, so applicants should compare the modules and the type of portfolio each course is designed to develop.

The campus has a distinctive visual character and spaces intended for creative work. Ask to see the studios, software and equipment used by the intended programme, and find out when students can access them. A film student, fashion student and animator need very different production resources, even when all three study within a creative environment.

Project costs deserve early attention. Printing, materials, model making, production, specialist software and a capable computer may sit outside tuition. Ask for typical briefs and recent student work, how feedback is delivered and whether collaboration across disciplines is part of the course. If an internship or industry project is promoted, confirm whether it is required, assessed and arranged by the university.

Before applying, confirm the current qualification title, teaching location, accreditation record and complete fee schedule. International students should use the programme's written requirements rather than a general institutional claim when checking future professional or postgraduate options. A strong creative decision rests on the course, facilities and body of work you can realistically produce.
