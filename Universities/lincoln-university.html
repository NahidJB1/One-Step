University overview

About Lincoln University College

Founded in 2002 as Lincoln College and upgraded to university college status in 2011, Lincoln University College (LUC) is a premier private higher education institution situated in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. LUC is dedicated to providing high-quality tertiary education with a strong emphasis on clinical training, professional practice, research, and career-focused innovation.

LUC is fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and approved by the Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE). The university has achieved a 5-Star SETARA rating and holds ISO 9001:2015 certification. Lincoln University College is ranked #701–710 in QS World University Rankings 2027. Lincoln University College is ranked #196 in QS Asia University Rankings 2026. LUC maintains active academic affiliations with the Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU) and International Association of Universities (IAU).

University perspective / LUC

Lincoln University College: check the route from study to professional practice

A broad portfolio includes healthcare courses where recognition and clinical arrangements need close attention.

Lincoln University College is based in Petaling Jaya and lists a wide range of qualifications across healthcare and other professional fields. A large course catalogue creates choice, but it also makes programme-level checking essential. Applicants should compare the exact award, entry requirements, study mode and current accreditation rather than apply on the strength of the university name alone.

The campus and teaching spaces provide a starting impression of student life. Clinical learning may also happen beyond the main campus, so medicine, pharmacy, nursing and allied-health applicants should ask where placements take place, how they are supervised and whether travel or equipment adds to the cost. Non-clinical students can make the same kind of check for labs, software and project facilities.

Professional recognition varies by qualification and country. A student hoping to practise after graduation should consult the relevant regulator in the intended destination as well as the university.
