About Multimedia University (MMU)

Multimedia University (MMU) is Malaysia's pioneer private university, established in 1996 by Telekom Malaysia (TM). Designed to serve as the technological engine for the country's Multimedia Super Corridor (MSC), MMU has evolved into a premier government-linked institution globally recognized for excellence in engineering, computing, creative multimedia, business, and law.

Awarded a 5-Star QS rating and recognized as a Premier Digital Tech Institution by MDEC, MMU fosters an innovative ecosystem where cutting-edge research intersects with hands-on industry collaboration. With a vibrant multicultural community and over 70,000 alumni worldwide, MMU continuously shapes digital leaders and tech entrepreneurs for the global economy.

Campus & Facilities

MMU features two flagship campuses in Cyberjaya and Melaka, each equipped with world-class academic, research, and recreational amenities designed to provide an inspiring university environment.

  • Siti Hasmah Digital Library: Advanced digital library facilities offering vast online journals, quiet study suites, and collaborative learning zones.
  • High-Tech Computing & AI Labs: Specialized laboratories equipped with NVIDIA AI workstations, high-speed fiber infrastructure, and cloud computing testbeds.
  • Cinematic & Multimedia Production Studios: Industry-standard soundstages, motion capture studios, green screen rooms, and sound editing suites.
  • Advanced Engineering Workshops: Specialized labs for robotics, telecommunications, microelectronics, and renewable energy innovation.
  • Sports & Recreational Complex: Olympic-size swimming pool, indoor sports hall, football field, gymnasium, and tennis courts.
  • On-Campus Student Housing: Secure, comfortable student residences with high-speed internet, air conditioning, and 24/7 security.

Faculties & Schools

  • Faculty of Computing and Informatics (FCI) - Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Cybersecurity & AI
  • Faculty of Engineering (FOE / FET) - Electrical, Electronics, Mechanical, Telecommunication & Mechatronics Engineering
  • Faculty of Creative Multimedia (FCM) - Animation, Visual Effects, Virtual Reality, Interface Design & Digital Media
  • Faculty of Management (FOM / FOB) - Business Administration, Accounting, Finance, Marketing & Management
  • Faculty of Law (FOL) - Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) and Legal Studies
  • Faculty of Cinematic Arts (FCA) - Film Production, Screenwriting & Digital Cinematography
  • Faculty of Applied Communication (FAC) - Strategic Communication, Corporate Comm & Media Studies
