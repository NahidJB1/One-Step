University overview

About Multimedia University

Multimedia University (MMU) is Malaysia's pioneer private university, established in 1996 by Telekom Malaysia (TM). Designed to serve as the technological engine for the country's Multimedia Super Corridor (MSC), MMU has evolved into a premier government-linked institution globally recognized for excellence in engineering, computing, creative multimedia, business, and law.

Awarded a 5-Star QS rating and recognized as a Premier Digital Tech Institution by MDEC, MMU fosters an innovative ecosystem where cutting-edge research intersects with hands-on industry collaboration. With a vibrant multicultural community and over 70,000 alumni worldwide, MMU continuously shapes digital leaders and tech entrepreneurs for the global economy.

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give Multimedia University (MMU) an overall QS rank?

When are MMU's intakes?

The FAQ lists March, July and October 2023. These dates are historical and should not be reused as current dates. Intakes and application deadlines can differ by programme and campus; confirm the current dates before arranging travel or a visa application.

What English score is required?

English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

Where is MMU located?

University perspective / MMU

MMU: compare Cyberjaya and Melaka before choosing the programme

Technology, engineering, creative media, business and law sit across a multi-campus university.

Multimedia University is closely linked with Malaysia's digital and telecommunications development. Its course list spans computing, engineering, creative multimedia, business and law, with study connected to the Cyberjaya and Melaka campuses. The campus is not a minor detail: it affects the facilities, accommodation, transport and student community surrounding a programme.

Cyberjaya is associated with a technology-oriented city environment, while Melaka offers a different scale and daily rhythm. Applicants should confirm where the full course is taught and whether any semester or activity takes place elsewhere. The gallery provides a view of campus life, libraries and student activities, but the offer letter should settle the actual teaching location.

For technical and creative programmes, compare labs, software, project work and final-year expectations. Engineering applicants should check programme-specific recognition; creative students can ask to see graduate work and production facilities. Law and business students should examine electives, internships and the relationship between academic study and professional requirements.

Use the course and fee data here to build a shortlist, then request the latest curriculum and payment schedule for the intended intake. Budget separately for housing and travel at the named campus. Rankings can add context, but course content, accreditation and the work students complete provide a better programme-level comparison.
