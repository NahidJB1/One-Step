About the University

SEGi University is one of the largest private education providers in Malaysia. Known for its iconic Roman-style architecture in Kota Damansara, it offers a complete learning ecosystem from Foundation to PhD levels.

SEGi is particularly renowned for its Health Sciences programs (Medicine, Dentistry, Optometry) and its diverse international community, making quality education accessible to all.

Our Locations

The flagship campus is located in Kota Damansara, directly connected to the MRT station, making it highly accessible. SEGi also has key college campuses in Subang, Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Sarawak.
