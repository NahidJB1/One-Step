Questions and answers

Frequently Asked Questions About SEGi University Malaysia

What is the difference between SEGi University and SEGi colleges?

They are distinct institutions within SEGi's education network. Check the teaching institution, campus and awarding body on the programme page and offer; their course availability and admissions information should not be mixed.

Which campus should I use when researching SEGi University?

Start with the university's Kota Damansara information and confirm the programme's actual teaching arrangements. A SEGi college location or partner award may require a separate comparison.

What is the QS World ranking of SEGi University in 2027?

SEGi University is ranked #701–710 in QS World University Rankings 2027.

Sources: SEGi University - QS profile · QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1)

What is the QS Asia ranking of SEGi University in 2026?

SEGi University is ranked #350 in QS Asia University Rankings 2026.

Sources: SEGi University - QS Asia 2026 result

What is the ranking of SEGi University in Malaysia?

SEGi University has Malaysia position #18–20 among the 32 institutions listed under Malaysia in QS World 2027, calculated from the published table. The world-rank band does not establish a more precise national order. This is a position within the QS World table, not a separate government ranking or a QS Asia position.

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give SEGi University an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Are SEGi degrees accredited?

SEGi is described as SEGi is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Its additional quality claim is unsourced in the FAQ. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+ for undergraduate study and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+ for postgraduate study. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

SEGi is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

Where is SEGi University located?

The FAQ says Damansara, about 12 kilometres from Kuala Lumpur. Use the campus stated on the offer letter because teaching locations and available programmes can differ.

University perspective / SEGi

SEGi University: separate the university from the wider college network

Kota Damansara is the university base, while the SEGi name also appears across distinct colleges and partner routes.

SEGi University is based in Kota Damansara, Petaling Jaya. The wider SEGi network also includes separately named colleges, so a course carrying the SEGi brand does not automatically belong to the university. This is particularly important where a programme mentions a partner or dual award. Read the full teaching institution, campus and qualification names before treating two listings as equivalent.

The Kota Damansara campus brings teaching, library and student facilities together in an urban location. The course range includes business, computing, engineering, education and health-related study. Photos of laboratories, the library, hall and dental clinic show different parts of the environment, although access to specialist facilities depends on the faculty and programme.

For a partner qualification, ask what each awarding body contributes and what appears on the final certificate. For a professional or clinical course, check accreditation, placement arrangements and the regulator's requirements in the country where you intend to work. These questions are more useful than assuming that a partner name or university ranking applies equally to every programme.

International students should compare the current written fee schedule with accommodation and transport around Kota Damansara. Confirm where every stage is taught, whether industrial or clinical training changes the timetable, and which additional costs are compulsory. The programme card on this page is a starting point; the final offer should settle the current intake and amount payable.
