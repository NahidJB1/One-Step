About Taylor's University

Taylor's University is ranked #272 in QS World University Rankings 2027. Famous for its stunning Lakeside Campus, it combines academic excellence with world-class facilities to produce high-achieving graduates.

With a legacy dating back to 1969, Taylor's offers a diverse range of programmes and is renowned for its industry partnerships and global connectivity.

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give Taylor's University an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Should I use Taylor's MYR or USD fee schedule?

Select the international-student schedule for your programme on Taylor's official fee page and follow its payment-currency instructions. Do not substitute a Malaysian-student schedule or treat an old conversion as today's exchange rate.

Are Taylor's College fees the same as Taylor's University fees?

Use the schedule for the institution and qualification in your offer. Foundation and pre-university pathways need particular care because College and University information should not be treated as interchangeable.

What are Taylor's University's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Where can I find the official Taylor's University fee chart?

One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the Taylor's University fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does Taylor's University offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.
