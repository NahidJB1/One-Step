University overview

About Taylor's University

Taylor's University is ranked #272 in QS World University Rankings 2027. Famous for its stunning Lakeside Campus, it combines academic excellence with world-class facilities to produce high-achieving graduates.

With a legacy dating back to 1969, Taylor's offers a diverse range of programmes and is renowned for its industry partnerships and global connectivity.

Frequently Asked Questions About Taylor's University

Do all Taylor's programmes start in the same intake?

Use the official key-intakes page for the selected qualification. A general intake month does not establish availability for every undergraduate, postgraduate or College programme.

Where can I compare Taylor's with another university?

What is the ranking of Taylor's University in Malaysia?

Sources: QS World University Rankings 2027 - official results spreadsheet (v1.1) · QS World University Rankings 2027 - release corrections

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give Taylor's University an overall QS rank?

QS Stars and SETARA are ratings, not numerical positions in a QS league table. A subject ranking applies to that subject, and a partner university rank belongs to the named partner. Check the institution, ranking system and edition before comparing.

Sources: QS Stars rating system

Are Taylor's degrees accredited?

Taylor's is described as the degrees are recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education and internationally. Recognition should be checked for the exact programme and intended country. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

When are Taylor's intakes?

The listed information includes January, March and July for foundation; January, March and August for diploma; March and August for bachelor's; and February and September for master's study. Intakes and application deadlines can differ by programme and campus; confirm the current dates before arranging travel or a visa application.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.0–5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+ for undergraduate study, and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+ for postgraduate study. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

Taylor's is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

Where is Taylor's University located?

The listed address is 1 Jalan Taylors, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor. Use the campus stated on the offer letter because teaching locations and available programmes can differ.

University perspective / Taylor's

Taylor's University: look beyond the Lakeside first impression

A distinctive campus and broad subject range deserve a close programme-level comparison.

Taylor's University is known for its Lakeside Campus in Subang Jaya. The lake, teaching buildings and shared spaces give the university a recognisable setting, but their value depends on the facilities used by the chosen faculty. Taylor's lists courses across hospitality, business, computing, design and health-related fields, each with a different pattern of practical work and assessment.

The campus can support a lively student routine, with study, teaching and social spaces in one environment. Applicants should go beyond photographs and ask how their own faculty uses laboratories, studios, kitchens or project rooms. A campus visit, virtual tour or current faculty guide can help show whether the spaces match the way the programme is actually taught.

University and subject rankings may help create a shortlist, but they do not describe every module or professional outcome. Compare the current curriculum, elective choices, internship arrangements and any overseas or partner component. For programmes tied to a regulated profession, check the specific qualification and the recognition required in the country where you plan to work.

The written offer remains the key planning document for an international student. Confirm the qualification title, duration, intake, tuition schedule and additional charges. Accommodation near the Lakeside Campus varies in price and travel time, so place a realistic housing budget beside the course costs before deciding.
