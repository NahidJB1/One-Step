About the University

University College MAIWP International (UCMI) is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Federal Territory Islamic Religious Council (MAIWP). Originally known as PICOMS, it has transformed into a university college dedicated to producing holistic professionals.

UCMI is renowned for its affordable, high-quality education, particularly in Nursing, Medical Sciences, and Management. It plays a vital role in providing educational opportunities to the B40 community through various aid programs.

Admission Requirements

Our goal is to make the admission process as smooth as possible for you. The requirements may vary slightly depending on the specific program, but general requirements include:

  • High School/Secondary School Transcripts
  • English Proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL) - waived for some countries
  • Valid Passport (for international students)
  • Medical Check-up Report
