About UCSI University

Established in 1986, UCSI University is one of Malaysia's premier private higher education institutions. Consistently ranked among the top 1% of universities globally in the QS World University Rankings (#269 globally), UCSI is recognized for its academic quality, research impact, and strong industry partnerships.

UCSI is particularly acclaimed for its Institute of Music—ranked among the top 50 music schools worldwide—as well as its prestigious Medicine, Pharmacy, and Architecture faculties. With a vibrant multicultural environment hosting students from over 110 countries, UCSI emphasizes real-world experience through Malaysia's largest university-industry network.

Campus & Facilities

UCSI operates state-of-the-art campuses across Malaysia. The main Kuala Lumpur campus in Cheras spans 20 acres with multi-storey academic complexes, while the Springhill campus in Negeri Sembilan serves as a specialized health sciences hub integrated with UCSI Hospital.

  • UCSI Hospital & Clinical Simulation Facilities
  • World-Class Sound Studios & Recital Halls
  • Design & Architecture Studios
  • Petrochemical, Anatomy & Biotechnology Laboratories
