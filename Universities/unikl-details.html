About Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL)

Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL) is Malaysia's leading technical university, established in 2002 and wholly owned by Majlis Amanah Rakyat (MARA), an agency under the Ministry of Rural and Regional Development. UniKL operates under a unique "One Institute, One Specialisation" framework, housing specialised institutes across Malaysia that combine high-level academic study with practical technical training.

With over 29,000 students across its specialized campuses, UniKL collaborates closely with major global industrial leaders—such as Airbus, Thales, BMW, and Microsoft—to ensure curricula remain at the forefront of industry needs. Graduates benefit from hands-on laboratory experiences, industry certifications, and top graduate employability rates nationwide.

Campus & Facilities

UniKL's campus network is designed around high-tech domain specialization. Each institute offers purpose-built industrial machinery, flight simulators, marine test basins, and digital innovation centers, providing students with realistic hands-on experience before entering the workforce.

  • Aviation Maintenance Hangar & Flight Simulator Training Center (UniKL MIAT)
  • Chemical & Bioengineering Laboratories (UniKL MICET)
  • Cybersecurity, AI, Multimedia & Animation Studios (UniKL MIIT)
  • Marine Engineering & Shipyard Simulation Center (UniKL MIMET)
  • Advanced Robotics & Industrial Automation Labs (UniKL MFI / BMI)
  • Clinical Skills Simulation Labs & Pharmacy Facilities (UniKL RCMP / MESTECH)
  • High-Speed Campus-wide Wi-Fi & Digital Resource Library
  • Sports Complexes, Gymnasium & On-Campus Residences

Faculties & Schools

  • UniKL MIAT: Malaysian Institute of Aviation Technology (Aeronautical & Maintenance Engineering)
  • UniKL MIIT: Malaysian Institute of Information Technology (Software Engineering, Cyber Security, Multimedia & Animation)
  • UniKL BMI: British Malaysian Institute (Electrical, Electronic & Telecommunications Engineering)
  • UniKL MFI: Malaysia France Institute (Industrial Automation, Robotics & Mechanical Engineering)
  • UniKL MICET: Malaysian Institute of Chemical & Bioengineering Technology (Chemical, Bioprocess & Polymer Engineering)
  • UniKL MIMET: Malaysian Institute of Marine Engineering Technology (Naval Architecture & Marine Tech)
  • UniKL MSI: Malaysian Spanish Institute (Automotive & Manufacturing Engineering Technology)
  • UniKL Business School (UBIS): International Business, Entrepreneurship, Accounting & Finance
  • UniKL RCMP & MESTECH: Royal College of Medicine Perak & Institute of Medical Science Technology (Medicine, Pharmacy & Biomedical Science)
  • UniKL MIDI: Malaysian Institute of Design and Manufacturing Technology (Product Design & Engineering)
