University overview

About Universiti Tun Abdul Razak

UNIRAZAK is one of Malaysia's premier private universities, named after the nation's second Prime Minister. It is famous for being a "Boutique University" that offers specialized, high-quality education in government, business, and education.

Home to the prestigious **Tun Abdul Razak School of Government (TARSOG)** and the **Bank Rakyat School of Business**, UNIRAZAK is the top choice for students aiming for careers in public policy, leadership, and corporate entrepreneurship.

Do QS Stars, SETARA or partner rankings give Universiti Tun Abdul Razak (UNIRAZAK) an overall QS rank?

What are UNIRAZAK's tuition fees for international students?

What are UNIRAZAK's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Does the UNIRAZAK fee chart show fees per semester or per year?

Use the exact period printed in each column. A first-year payment, fee per semester and full-program total describe different costs. Where a semester or annual breakdown is absent, request a payment schedule; dividing a program total can give the wrong instalment amount.

Where can I find the official UNIRAZAK fee chart?

Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. Which year do the UNIRAZAK fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does UNIRAZAK offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.

University perspective / UNIRAZAK

UNIRAZAK: a focused choice for business, government and education

Programme structure, delivery mode and career direction are the useful points of comparison at this Kuala Lumpur university.

Universiti Tun Abdul Razak is commonly known as UNIRAZAK. Its listed programmes concentrate on areas such as business, management, government, education and postgraduate research. That focus can help students build a clear shortlist, but similar course titles may differ in specialisation, assessment and delivery mode. Read the module list before choosing by name alone.

UNIRAZAK offers a city-based study setting in Kuala Lumpur. The surrounding location can be convenient for professional events and services, while accommodation and commuting costs need their own planning. The gallery shows discussion areas, a library, hall and shared campus spaces; applicants should ask which facilities and class format apply to the intended programme.

Students interested in public policy, leadership or corporate study should look at how theory connects with practice. Ask whether the course uses case studies, industry projects, internships or research work, and how those activities are assessed. Postgraduate applicants should confirm supervision, research expectations and the frequency of on-campus sessions before making travel or work arrangements.

International applicants should request the current intake, entry requirements and complete written fee schedule. If the course is offered in a flexible, blended or weekend format, confirm that the mode is available and suitable under the student-pass arrangement. Check the exact qualification in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and use the final offer letter for payment decisions.
