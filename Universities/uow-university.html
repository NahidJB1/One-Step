University overview

About University of Wollongong Malaysia

Established in 2004, University of Wollongong (UOW) has grown into a dynamic private university focused on delivering career-oriented education. Originally founded as the Masterskill School of Nursing and Healthcare, UOW achieved full university status in 2012 and expanded its academic portfolio into Business Management, Information Technology, Computer Science, and Allied Health Sciences.

Fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognized internationally by the Accreditation Service for International Schools, Colleges & Universities (ASIC), UOW provides industry-focused diplomas, bachelor's degrees, and postgraduate qualifications designed to equip graduates with practical skills for the modern workforce.

University perspective / UOW

UOW Malaysia: match the programme to its Malaysian campus

An Australian university connection adds context, while the local award, location and course structure decide the experience.

UOW Malaysia is connected with the University of Wollongong in Australia and developed from the Malaysian institutions formerly associated with KDU. The local course list includes foundation, diploma, degree and postgraduate options across areas such as business, computing, communication, design and hospitality. Applicants should check the exact Malaysian institution and award for the programme they choose.

UOW Malaysia uses more than one campus location, including settings in the Klang Valley and Penang. A programme's campus affects the available facilities, local housing and travel. Confirm the full teaching address before making accommodation plans, especially when the same broad subject appears in course information for different locations.

The parent university's reputation may be useful background, but it does not mean every Malaysian programme has the same curriculum, award or ranking. Ask whether the qualification is issued locally, jointly or by the Australian university, and whether any transfer or study-abroad option is guaranteed or conditional. Compare the complete cost of each route.

Request the latest programme outline, intake and written fee schedule before applying. Hospitality, creative and technical courses may require specialist facilities, projects or placements, so ask how those are organised. The offer letter should settle the campus, duration and awarding body used for the student's actual route.
