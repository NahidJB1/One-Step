University overview

About Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka

Established in 2004, Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) has grown into a dynamic private university focused on delivering career-oriented education. Originally founded as the Masterskill School of Nursing and Healthcare, UTEM achieved full university status in 2012 and expanded its academic portfolio into Business Management, Information Technology, Computer Science, and Allied Health Sciences.

Fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognized internationally by the Accreditation Service for International Schools, Colleges & Universities (ASIC), UTEM provides industry-focused diplomas, bachelor's degrees, and postgraduate qualifications designed to equip graduates with practical skills for the modern workforce.

What are UTeM's tuition fees for international students?

Find your program and campus in the fee chart on this page, then read the relevant student category, fee columns and remarks. The schedule uses labels including Total Tuition Fees. Different programs and fee periods cannot be combined into one reliable tuition price. Amounts not shown need an intake-specific quotation.

What are UTeM's tuition fees for Bangladeshi students?

Bangladeshi applicants should check the international-student rate for their program and intake. This page does not establish a separate Bangladesh-only tuition rate or guarantee eligibility for a discount. Check nationality-specific charges, deposits, visa costs and scholarship conditions in the schedule and your written offer.

Does the UTeM fee chart show fees per semester or per year?

Use the exact period printed in each column. A first-year payment, fee per semester and full-program total describe different costs. Where a semester or annual breakdown is absent, request a payment schedule; dividing a program total can give the wrong instalment amount.

Where can I find the official UTeM fee chart?

The download links on this page open the stored fee documents. One-Step is a student guidance website. Confirm the document's edition, campus, student category and validity with the university before accepting an offer or paying. The university's current written fee schedule and offer determine the amount payable.

Which year do the UTeM fees apply to?

Use any effective dates and intake restrictions stated in the fee documents and course remarks. A single validity year has not been confirmed for every table on this page. A recent page update does not mean all underlying fees are for the current year.

Does UTeM offer scholarships for international students?

Check any scholarship, adjusted tuition or discount columns alongside their conditions. A listed reduction is not an automatic award for every international or Bangladeshi applicant. Ask for the scholarship name, eligibility, application deadline, renewal conditions and final payable fee in writing.

Are UTeM degrees accredited?

UTeM is described as UTeM is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Individual programme records should be checked separately. Accreditation applies to the individual qualification, so confirm the exact programme in the Malaysian Qualifications Register and on the current offer letter.

What IELTS or TOEFL score is required?

The listed requirement is IELTS 5.0–5.5 or TOEFL iBT 46+ for undergraduate study and IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60+ for postgraduate study. English requirements can be higher for particular programmes or professional fields, and the university must confirm which tests and score editions it accepts.

Which registration documents are requested?

A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate. The university may also request certified academic records, translations, English-language evidence or additional visa documents after reviewing the application.

Is on-campus accommodation available?

UTeM is described as yes. A room is not guaranteed until it is confirmed in writing, so check availability, room type, rent, deposit and included utilities for the intended intake.

What off-campus housing is available?

It mentions nearby condominiums, hostels and houses, with agency assistance available. Compare the tenancy term, deposit, utilities, travel time and transport before committing to a private room.

Where is UTeM located?

Jalan Hang Tuah Jaya, 76100 Durian Tunggal, Melaka. Use the campus stated on the offer letter because teaching locations and available programmes can differ.

University perspective / UTeM

UTeM: technical study with an applied engineering focus

A public technical university in Melaka where laboratories, projects and industrial training shape the course.

Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka is a public technical university commonly known as UTeM. Its listed undergraduate programmes are concentrated in engineering, engineering technology, computing and related applied fields. Students comparing UTeM should look closely at the specialisation, because similar engineering titles can lead to different laboratory work and professional paths.

The university is based in Melaka rather than the Klang Valley. Its campus environment includes academic buildings, laboratories, library facilities and student transport. The location may offer a different living pattern from Kuala Lumpur, and applicants should compare accommodation and travel with the actual timetable and faculty location.

Applied technical education is best understood through what students do. Ask about laboratory hours, design projects, workshops, software, industrial training and the final-year project. Engineering applicants should also check programme-specific accreditation and how the qualification relates to professional registration in Malaysia and the country where they hope to work.

Before accepting an offer, confirm the international entry requirements, intake, tuition and any compulsory equipment or project costs. The fee information on this page is a planning reference; the university's current written offer should determine payment. Students who expect an internship should ask how placements are arranged and whether location creates additional housing or travel costs.
