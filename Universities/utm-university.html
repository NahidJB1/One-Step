About Universiti Teknologi Malaysia

Established in 2004, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) has grown into a dynamic private university focused on delivering career-oriented education. Originally founded as the Masterskill School of Nursing and Healthcare, UTM achieved full university status in 2012 and expanded its academic portfolio into Business Management, Information Technology, Computer Science, and Allied Health Sciences.

Fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognized internationally by the Accreditation Service for International Schools, Colleges & Universities (ASIC), UTM provides industry-focused diplomas, bachelor's degrees, and postgraduate qualifications designed to equip graduates with practical skills for the modern workforce.

University perspective / UTM

UTM: compare the Johor Bahru and Kuala Lumpur study settings

Engineering and technology lead a broad public-university portfolio across two very different campuses.

Universiti Teknologi Malaysia is a public research university widely known as UTM. Engineering, technology and the built environment are central to its identity, while its programme list also includes computing, science, management, education and postgraduate research. Applicants should compare the specific faculty and curriculum rather than treat every technical degree as interchangeable.

UTM's large main campus is in Johor Bahru, and the university also has a Kuala Lumpur campus. The two settings create different choices for housing, transport and access to industry. Confirm where the full programme is delivered before applying; a university-wide description or photograph does not establish that a particular course is available at both campuses.

For engineering and professional subjects, check programme accreditation, laboratory work, industrial training and later registration requirements. Research students should investigate supervisor fit, facilities and project funding. Rankings offer a broad measure of the institution, but the department, modules and access to equipment will shape the day-to-day academic experience.

International applicants should verify the current application route, intake, tuition and living arrangements. Build a budget for the named campus, including accommodation and local travel. The final written offer should state the qualification, location, duration and fees used for payment and student-pass planning.
