A personal approach to studying abroad

Founded in 2024, OneStepMy helps students from around the world explore and pursue study opportunities in Malaysia. Our website uses the One-Step name. We welcome international students from every background and offer dedicated support for students applying from Bangladesh.

Support from your first question to arrival

We guide students through university and course selection, application preparation and the steps involved in starting their studies. Our support continues in Malaysia with airport pickup, accommodation assistance, medical screening coordination and university registration, as arranged with our team.

We also offer job-search guidance, subject to students’ eligibility and the relevant rules. Visit our Student support in Malaysia page for the full service descriptions, preparation steps and arrangements to confirm before travelling.

Clear information. Transparent processing.

We believe students should understand what is happening with their application and what they are paying for. We explain the steps, distinguish university charges from any OneStepMy processing fee, and help students check the requirements that apply to their chosen institution.

Our website resources and counselling are free. We receive commissions from some partner universities. If a student chooses an institution that requires additional processing support and does not pay recruitment commissions, a OneStepMy processing fee may apply. We explain the applicable amount for that institution. The fee is payable only when the student receives the visa. See our processing-fee terms for details.

Connections across Malaysian universities

The institutions listed in our university directory are OneStepMy partners. Explore their courses, campus information and published fee schedules, then speak with us about the options that fit your study plans. Admission, scholarships and visa decisions remain with the relevant institutions and authorities.

Your story stays yours

Students trust us with personal documents, questions and conversations. We keep these private and use application information for the processing and support students request. We do not publish private conversations or use student documents, photographs or success stories for marketing without the student’s explicit permission.

Looking ahead

Malaysia is our current study destination focus. We are working towards UK registration and exploring expansion there. This is a future plan; it does not mean that OneStepMy is already registered or operating in the UK.
