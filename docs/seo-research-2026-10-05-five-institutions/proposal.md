# One-Step: five-institution SEO research and implementation proposal

**Phase 1 completed 6 October 2026.** Research began 5 October. Exactly ten existing pages: ALFA, Taylor's University, SEGi University, City University Malaysia and INTI, one fees page and one details page each. No website content, code, fee data, live files, settings or Search Console submissions changed in this phase. Research files only.

Read with [baseline and raw-export limitations](baseline.md), [technical audit](technical-audit.md), [ALFA/CityU/INTI research](research-alfa-cityu-inti.md), [Taylor's/SEGi research](research-taylors-segi.md) and [source register](sources.md).

## Diagnosis

**The strongest immediate opportunity is to make existing fee traffic useful and trustworthy, then improve specific course/campus answers. Broad discovery failure is not the main demonstrated problem.** All ten pages return 200, are self-canonical, occur in the sitemap and received recorded impressions in the latest complete 28 days. September's differentiated metadata, static fee guidance, crosslinks and rankings are already live; rebuilding that work would waste effort.

The latest exact-ten window, 6 September–3 October, has **65 clicks / 5,211 impressions**, versus **84 / 5,418** during 9 August–5 September. Fees pages produced 63 of the 65 recorded clicks. Details-page exposure rose from 224 to 1,202 impressions, but produced only two clicks. This supports different hypotheses: fees need accurate, competitive answers and a working enquiry path; most details pages still need stronger relevance/competitiveness before a snippet change can have much effect. Taylor's details averages position 27.5, making a “low CTR means bad title” diagnosis especially weak.

There are three confirmed decision-quality defects: **Taylor's and INTI amounts require source-edition reconciliation; SEGi's map points to Taylor's; and the shared contact form reports success without submitting.** The last is a critical dependency for the enquiry objective, outside the ten landing pages. Traffic cannot compensate for a non-delivering form. Conversion performance is unknown, because no completed-enquiry analytics or CRM evidence was available.

The prior runtime clean-link/double-slash patch is local-only according to the live asset comparison. That warrants a separately authorized deployment and native-server verification, not new redirect-chain work. Historical `.html` impressions are not sufficient evidence of current duplicate indexing or a penalty.

**Effect of September's changes remains unproven.** Only eight post-deployment days are present in the latest complete data. Recent details exposure is encouraging, but mixed periods, query mix and canonical attribution prevent causal claims. No ranking or traffic forecast is offered.

## Highest-priority actions and decision rules

### 1. Repair the enquiry dependency before expecting lead growth — P0, separately approved shared scope

**Evidence:** all ten pages link to `/contact`; its live form cancels submission and shows an alert. No confirmed lead tracking exists. **Change:** choose an honest interim WhatsApp handoff or implement a real acknowledged enquiry flow; define completion/qualification events. **Mechanism:** interested students can actually reach One-Step, and completed enquiries become measurable. **Metric:** successful stored/delivered enquiries, failure rate, qualified-enquiry count; outbound contact clicks separately. **Decision rule:** do not accept the form as repaired until an authorized test is received and failure states cannot show success. Never infer completion from a button click. **Risk/verification:** shared flow affects other pages; test delivery, duplicates, validation, spam control and privacy. No messages or test submissions were sent during research.

This is outside the ten landing pages and is not silently included in their implementation approval. It is the strongest challenge to the assumption that more organic visits alone will increase enquiries.

### 2. Reconcile fee evidence before expanding fee claims — P1

**Evidence:** ALFA/SEGi lack confirmed current editions; CityU has a conflicting edition lead; Taylor's/INTI have direct official-source discrepancies. **Change:** attach source issuer, document/link, publication/validity dates when known, institution/campus, international category, currency, fee period, tuition versus total, payment stages, inclusions/exclusions and verification status to affected sections. Update amounts only against a reviewed exact source. **Mechanism:** a useful, trustworthy answer to commercial-intent searches and fewer misleading comparisons. **Metric:** 100% traceability for changed rows, zero introduced source mismatches, fee-cluster clicks and qualified enquiries after deployment. **Decision rule:** publish a numerical change only when its dimensions and notes match the source; unresolved sections get an honest unconfirmed-edition label, not fabricated current-year certainty. Keep correctness fixes even if traffic is inconclusive. **Risk/verification:** totals/tax/double counting and PDF/table mismatches; compare every edited cell/note/download and preserve filters/anchors.

Start with Taylor's fee interpretation because it has the most recorded exposure, INTI because the discrepancy is concrete, and ALFA because it already supplies most clicks. This ordering is opportunity-based, not a prediction of future traffic.

### 3. Correct location/identity and make selected programme decisions concrete — P1, targeted details work

**Evidence:** SEGi wrong map, ALFA conflicting records, CityU regional-centre/grouped-fee ambiguity, INTI university/college distinction; current programme examples mostly come from fee data. **Change:** correct factual errors and replace selected generic examples with compact, sourced institution/course/location/mode/award/intake rows. **Mechanism:** better long-tail relevance and a clearer route from course choice to fees. **Metric:** relevant course/campus impressions, query-to-page ownership, details→fee-section transitions and qualified enquiries. **Decision rule:** expand only where source quality and query/enquiry evidence justify maintenance; if fees queries surface on details, first improve the link to the fee owner rather than duplicate tuition tables. **Risk/verification:** programme availability and accreditation conflation; verify each source, test destinations and mobile table layout, retain college/university distinctions.

### 4. Verify deployment of the existing URL patch — P1 dependency, separate release authorization

**Evidence:** current live JavaScript still uses `.html` builders and generated Home `..//`; duplicate-slash paths return 200. **Change:** review and deploy the previously prepared local patch, with native Hostinger redirect verification; no new redirect-chain optimization. **Mechanism:** consistent internal canonical signals and fewer duplicate crawl paths. **Metric:** rendered canonical-link coverage, permanent redirects/query preservation, subsequent Google-selected canonical and family-level impressions. **Decision rule:** ship only if canonical pages/assets/APIs/legacy aliases still work without loops; falling historical exclusion counts are not the sole success criterion. **Risk:** shared routing, encoded paths and server-specific behaviour. Existing local model tests do not certify production rewrite behaviour.

### 5. Establish conversion evidence and run one justified snippet experiment — P1 measurement, P2 experiment

**Evidence:** no verified enquiry count; marginal GSC exports are partial; Taylor's has exposure but lower clicks and position changes. **Change:** resolve event/CRM definitions and export inconsistencies, then test a factual description on Taylor's fees separately from its data release. Keep well-aligned titles initially. **Mechanism:** identify whether more relevant clicks lead to useful contacts, rather than optimize a headline CTR detached from intent. **Metric:** equivalent query/country/device/position-band CTR, organic qualified enquiries, fee-to-contact transitions. **Decision rule:** if position or query/device mix shifts materially, do not attribute CTR changes to wording; if uncertainty intervals overlap or events are scarce, extend to 56/84 days. A factual rewrite can remain for clarity without claiming a measured search lift. **Risk:** simultaneous changes obscure attribution; Google may choose a different snippet.

## Competitiveness and authority

Official pages have an inherent advantage for institution identity and current programme facts. Independent competitors already offer programme filters, fee breakdowns, dates and localized currencies. One-Step can be more useful by making the **original payment basis and source edition understandable**, resolving institution/campus ambiguities and connecting course choice to an appropriately qualified enquiry. More generic prose, schema, an unsupported “2027” label or repeated title rewrites will not create that advantage.

A backlink/authority deficit is plausible for broad institution queries, but **not measured**: no current referring-domain inventory, GSC Links export or credible competitor link comparison was obtained. Do not assign a domain-authority score or claim link building is certainly the main bottleneck.

Legitimate later opportunities: an official adviser/agent directory listing where a real authorized relationship exists; a jointly checked fee-reading resource an institution can choose to cite; consented student accounts of campus selection/arrival with dated first-hand evidence; a maintained comparison of fee periods and included charges with transparent methodology; Bangladesh school/counsellor workshops if that audience is confirmed. Use genuine editorial relevance and disclosure. No outreach, link purchases, fabricated testimonials or guaranteed placements are proposed or performed. Measure relevant referring pages, referral enquiries and assisted qualified enquiries; do not equate link count with success.

## Measurement and decision plan

1. **Before any deployment:** freeze the newest complete exact-ten and URL-family exports; latest saved windows are 6 September–3 October and 9 August–5 September, with 4 July–3 October context. Resolve partial country/device exports, collect page-filtered queries and confirm priority geography. Record current index status, Google-selected canonical and crawl date for all ten. Capture source versions and completed-enquiry baseline; mark unknowns explicitly.
2. **Deployment annotation D:** record local commit/file hashes, approved scope, actual production timestamp, each fee-source edition, description variants, image/shared changes, tracking activation and test outcome. Local generation is not deployment. Exclude deployment day from comparisons if its time is mixed. Record later Google recrawl dates separately; do not start the traffic clock at local implementation.
3. **Around D+28:** compare D+1…D+28 against D−28…D−1 after data is complete. Inspect each page and aggregate fees/details, exact canonical and family view, country/device, query intent and position bands. Check technical regressions and enquiry delivery immediately; do not wait 28 days to repair a failure.
4. **Around D+56:** compare days 29–56 with days 1–28 and the unchanged pre-period. Evaluate source accuracy, programme/campus query alignment and enquiry quality. Extend sparse cohorts rather than making frequent copy changes.
5. **Around D+84:** compare days 57–84 with earlier equivalent windows; include the prior-year same-intake period or six-month trend if available. Decide keep/iterate/expand based on relevant clicks and qualified enquiries, not total impressions alone.

For the existing 26 September release, approximate review checkpoints are **24 October, 21 November and 19 December 2026**, with reporting after complete data arrives. A new deployment needs its own annotation/cohort. The historical 28 August–24 September baseline is retained as recorded; it is not silently substituted for 29 August–25 September when applying the above D-relative formula.

Define enquiry stages explicitly: (a) organic landing visit, (b) fee/details navigation or PDF download, (c) contact/WhatsApp click, (d) **received and acknowledged enquiry**, (e) staff-qualified enquiry for one of the five institutions, with relevant study level/programme/intake and a usable contact route. A WhatsApp click is not a message, a form alert is not receipt, and an application account is not automatically a qualified enquiry. Count repeat enquiries consistently and exclude testing/spam. Keep personal details out of analytics payloads.

Decision rules: use absolute counts alongside rates; analyse equivalent query/device/country groups and position ranges. Report CTR/conversion uncertainty intervals where meaningful. If counts are sparse, intervals overlap materially, the query mix changes or tracking was absent, label the result **inconclusive** and extend the window. Zero clicks from a handful of impressions is not failure. If exposure improves but no qualified enquiries appear, inspect intent and the contact journey before adding content. If corrected source information reduces unsuitable enquiries, total contact count can fall while quality improves. Observational before/after evidence does not establish causation even when the direction is favourable.
