# ALFA, City University Malaysia and INTI: research and proposed scope

Research date: **5 October 2026**, user timezone Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Phase 1 only. This report changes no website content or data. Bangladesh is the existing editorial audience, but remains **provisional for this proposal pending the user's confirmation**. This is part of the five-institution study; live technical and Search Console results are recorded separately by the main audit.

## Method and limits

Inspected `docs/seo-university-intent.md`, the relevant entries of `data/university_seo_content.json`, the six local fee/details files, fee records in `data/fees_data.json`, and the dated `docs/search-console-2026-09-26.md` record. The latter is historical evidence, not a current performance measurement.

Used ordinary web search and opened official, authority and comparison pages. **No location-controlled Google SERP, fixed device, Google ranking position, paid keyword volume or traffic estimate was available.** Search location was not controlled. English-language queries included Malaysia and Bangladesh qualifiers where useful. Being returned in this search is evidence of discoverable competing content, not evidence of a particular Google position in Bangladesh. All query groups below are candidates to validate against current GSC queries/enquiries.

Official websites can contain inconsistent or old information. Programme registration codes, accreditation, fee validity and admission availability are separate facts. Do not infer that a printed registration renewal month means accreditation has expired, or that a published intake month means applications are currently open.

## What already exists and should be preserved

The September implementation already created distinct fee/details titles and descriptions, scope notes, fee interpretation, conditional scholarship wording, Bangladesh assessment guidance, programme examples, sources, reciprocal links and fee-section anchors. These are not new recommendations. The new opportunity is to **make selected information more concrete and resolve source conflicts**, without adding another layer of generic guidance.

`data/university_seo_content.json` is the editorial source for these generated sections; `scripts/university_seo_content.js` renders them. Fee values come from `data/fees_data.json`; City University's key there is `city-university`, while the public URL/editorial key is `cityu`. Preserve this mapping. Titles should not be rewritten merely because this is a new audit.

The September 26 report describes August 28–September 24 Bangladesh results: ALFA fees 5 clicks/94 impressions; details 1/22; CityU fees 0/3 and details 0/36; INTI fees 0/12 and details 0/10. These tiny dated samples do not establish current demand, CTR problems or the effect of the September deployment. Missing rows never mean zero demand or non-indexing.

## Institution evidence and bounded opportunities

### ALFA

**Identity:** The current official site and [MQR institution register](https://www.mqa.gov.my/mqr/english/eakrKPList_print.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=211) use **Alfa University College (AUC)**, previously ALFA College. Use University College, not an invented full-university status. [Official contact](https://alfa.edu.my/contact-us/) gives 13, Persiaran Kewajipan, USJ 1, Subang Jaya. MQR's institutional address still points to Subang Square: use the current official contact for travel planning and the register for programme accreditation; do not silently conflate them.

**Courses/intakes:** The [Diploma in Business Administration](https://alfa.edu.my/diploma-in-business-administration/) page lists full-time study, 2.5 years and January/May/September. Its printed course code ends 01/26. MQR lists the qualification as MQA/FA8031 with accreditation starting 25 October 2019. A stale printed course-renewal month is a verification trigger, not proof the course is unaccredited. The [programme directory](https://alfa.edu.my/category/programmes/page/2/) contains other programme-specific information, which should be checked individually.

**Conflict:** [General international admissions](https://alfa.edu.my/international-students/) states IELTS 5.0/equivalent for diploma study; the Diploma Business page states 5.5/equivalent. Do not introduce either as a universal Bangladesh requirement. The current One-Step wording requiring individual assessment is appropriate. A proposed programme matrix can show qualification, mode, duration and source while leaving disputed entry conditions explicitly unconfirmed.

**Fees:** Local ALFA has no top-level validity field. It distinguishes Foundation Resource Fee/Sem from other resource columns and separates RM6,000 initial charges. [An independently hosted schedule](https://iwiseeducation.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ALFA-1.pdf) explicitly says revised 2025; matching some values does not prove the local document is current for a 2026/2027 intake. [Another agent-hosted PDF](https://studyaviators.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/ALFA-UNIVERSITY-FEES-STRUCTURE-2025.pdf) also labels 2025. These are leads to identify an edition, not authority for new fee claims. Request/verify the institution-issued schedule and whether tax/resource charges are included before changing amounts.

**Competitor comparison:** [Shiksha fees](https://www.shiksha.com/studyabroad/malaysia/universities/alfa-university-college/fees) emphasizes Indian students, INR conversions and first-year amounts. [EasyUni courses](https://www.easyuni.my/malaysia/alfa-university-college-10213/courses/) adds filters, intakes and estimated annual local/foreign amounts. [GAC's ALFA page](https://www.gaconnt.com/university/alfa-university-college) targets MYR/BDT and entry amounts. Their figures are not interchangeable or automatically reliable. One-Step's useful alternative is a traceable row-level explanation of original fee basis and first-payment inclusions, plus verified course-to-fee navigation. Do not create more programme URLs within this 10-page scope.

**Incremental content proposal:** Replace unsubstantiated legacy phrases such as lecturers knowing every student by name, “particularly famous”, “award-winning” or the REAL Kids association unless documented. Add a concise accreditation panel naming two or three genuinely verified programme records and a dated source review. Retain the existing University College title and fee/detail split. Exact draft:

> ALFA University College is based in Subang Jaya. Before comparing courses, match the exact qualification and study mode to the official programme page and Malaysian Qualifications Register. The register records accreditation for individual qualifications; it does not confirm that applications are open for your chosen intake.

Fee draft, only after inspecting the stored PDF edition:

> **Schedule edition and fee basis:** This chart reproduces the edition identified beside each section. A Year 1 amount is not the full programme cost. Resource charges and initial international-student fees use separate labels; ask for a written payment schedule showing which charges are already included before paying.

The second passage should **replace/consolidate** existing similar guidance, not duplicate it. Add actual source edition beside the existing download when proven; otherwise “Schedule validity has not been confirmed for your intake.” No automatic 2026/2027 badge.

### City University Malaysia

**Identity/location:** The [official homepage](https://city.edu.my/) and [contact page](https://city.edu.my/about/contact-us/) identify Malaysian locations; the contact page gives Petaling Jaya and a specific Cyberjaya address. The homepage labels Kota Kinabalu a **Regional Centre**, despite using “four campuses” in surrounding marketing. Describe that location precisely; do not imply every fee-chart programme is available there. The current fee chart covers Johor Bahru and PJ/Cyberjaya sections, not a Sabah schedule.

**Programme example:** The [official MBA page](https://city.edu.my/master-of-business-administration/) specifies Petaling Jaya, coursework, 12 months, full/part-time study and Jan/Feb, May/June, Sept/Oct intakes, with reference MQA/FA11766 and a 08/2030 code. These are published programme facts, not a confirmation that the next intake has seats or that each mode is available to an international student. The [Education Malaysia listing](https://educationmalaysia.gov.my/city-university-malaysia/master-of-business-administration) retains a code ending 08/2025. Prefer current programme evidence and verify MQR rather than copying the older code. Do not generalize its English/entry conditions to all courses.

**Ranking:** [QS publisher profile](https://www.topuniversities.com/universities/city-university-malaysia) supports =558 in QS Asia 2026. The existing page already says this. It is not a QS World position and needs no inflation or repetition.

**Fees:** Local `city-university` data explicitly says 1 January–31 December 2026. Its MBA row has published fees RM52,000, scholarship tuition RM22,403 and total RM35,523, with separate resource and initial-fee notes. [Official international admissions](https://city.edu.my/admission-scholarships/international-students/) directs applicants to the International Marketing Office/representatives for current schedules. [Neogoals MBA](https://neogoals.com/en/universities/city-university-malaysia/programs/master-of-business-administration-mba) describes a different February–December 2026 promotion/fee edition with different amounts and eligibility conditions. This is a **conflict to resolve**, not permission to import a competitor's numbers. No new totals or tax arithmetic are proposed until the issuer confirms the precise schedule and inclusions.

**Competitor comparison:** Official MBA content supplies actual mode, location, subjects and intakes. Neogoals offers line-item fee interpretation, exclusions, edition and direct programme enquiries. One-Step already has broad tables and reciprocal links; it needs a traceable, reconciled source and programme-specific payment explanation, not another generic “affordable” paragraph. For details, a compact programme/campus matrix would answer “MBA at which campus?” better than a gallery and broad faculty list.

**Incremental wording proposal:** Replace legacy generic reputation prose and clarify the regional centre. Draft:

> **Choose the programme and teaching location together.** City University Malaysia publishes information for Petaling Jaya, Cyberjaya and Johor Bahru, and identifies Kota Kinabalu as a regional centre. The MBA programme page currently names Petaling Jaya; the fee chart groups some schedules under PJ/Cyberjaya. Confirm the location on your offer rather than assuming that a grouped fee section establishes teaching availability at both locations.

Link “MBA fee section and payment notes” to `/Fees_Chart/cityu-fees#fee-category-3`. Reverse link: “Check City University Malaysia programmes and teaching locations” to `/Universities/cityu-details#courses-intakes`. That relationship already exists generically; improve the anchor specificity only where relevant. Keep the public `cityu` slug and the underlying `city-university` alias.

### INTI

**Identity/campuses:** [Official directory](https://newinti.edu.my/campuses/) distinguishes **INTI International University, Nilai**, **INTI International College Subang**, **INTI International College Penang**, and INTI College Sabah. The six-page study here uses the existing One-Step umbrella page and its Nilai/Subang/Penang fee sections; do not claim the fee table covers Sabah. Directory/campus labels are more useful than treating all sites as one university campus. [Nilai's official page](https://newinti.edu.my/campuses/inti-international-university/) supports its residential-campus description.

**Ranking:** [QS publisher profile](https://www.topuniversities.com/universities/inti-international-university) currently supports =406 in QS World 2027, already reflected locally. Ranking belongs to the named university, not automatically a partner award or college course. Retain existing caution; avoid a needless ranking rewrite.

**Clear current-source discrepancy:** [Official Diploma in Computer Science](https://newinti.edu.my/programme/diploma-in-computer-science/) supplies separate international fee figures by campus and an SST exclusion note. Local rows versus official published approximations on 2026-10-05 are:

| Campus | Local Total Tuition Fee (RM) | Official international fee table (approximate; excludes SST) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Nilai | 42,640 | 46,900 |
| Subang | 44,380 | 48,820 |
| Penang | 39,000 | 42,900 |

The official page also links programme-specific campus intake information and identifies separate accreditation references. This shows the current One-Step schedule needs reconciliation; it does **not** establish that every local amount is wrong or that the official approximation equals a complete payable total. Preserve the old source edition and obtain exact current component schedules. The [official application guide](https://newinti.edu.my/international-students/how-to-apply/) names RM2,847 in an administrative/EMGS-related note, versus local RM2,520. Verify scope, date, nationality and tax before substitution. Do not copy financial bank details into the SEO content.

**Bangladesh:** [Official international entry page](https://newinti.edu.my/international-students/entry-requirements/) lists Bangladesh SSC/HSC qualification names, without a universal admission score or direct-entry guarantee. This supports a useful official link; preserve programme/campus-level assessment. No intake currently open is established by the repeating month lists.

**Competitor comparison:** [EasyUni Subang courses](https://www.easyuni.my/malaysia/inti-international-college-subang-202/courses/) offers filters, intake and annual-cost framing. [GoUni Diploma CS](https://gouni.my/courses/diploma-in-computer-science-10/) labels a source verification date, fee basis and tax exclusion. [Neogoals Nilai programme directory](https://neogoals.com/en/universities/inti-international-university/programs) explicitly identifies its 2025 edition; newer page formatting is not proof of newer tuition. One-Step should make version, campus, awarding institution and exclusions equally obvious while avoiding unverified competitor eligibility assertions.

**Incremental wording proposal:** Keep the fees title, which already owns the right intent. On details, replace “widest range” and unsupported superlatives with a compact comparison:

> **Which INTI institution offers your course?** Nilai is home to INTI International University. Subang and Penang are separate INTI colleges. Select the programme, teaching institution and awarding body together; a similar course name does not establish the same award, intake or fee.

Add a matrix with campus, exact institution, selected course, awarding body, official course source and link to the correct fee section. Include only directly verified rows; no broad copied catalogue. The existing generic programme-examples block is the place to improve, rather than appending another block. Fee links: Penang `/Fees_Chart/inti-fees#fee-category-0`; Nilai `#fee-category-1`; Subang `#fee-category-2`. Reverse link: `/Universities/inti-details#courses-intakes`.

## What this research deliberately does not establish

No numerical search volume, Google rank in Bangladesh, current enquiry conversion rate, present indexing state, current intake seat availability, fee validity beyond the supplied editions, scholarship entitlement, universal Bangladesh grade/English threshold, or causal improvement from prior SEO edits is established here. Current official rankings for INTI/CityU corroborate existing local content; no ranking expansion is needed. ALFA's promotional five-star/employment claims were not accepted as independent ranking evidence.

No files outside this research report were edited. No institution, representative or student was contacted.
