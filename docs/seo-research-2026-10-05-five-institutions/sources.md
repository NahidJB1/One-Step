## Reproducibility and unknowns

No location-controlled Google ranking audit, keyword-volume service, enquiry/CRM export, full backlink inventory, field CWV measurement or current open-intake confirmation was obtained. Numerical traffic projections are intentionally absent. Source review dates indicate observation, not the validity of a fee schedule. Publication dates remain those of the issuer; there is no automatic freshness date.

Research collectors write only within this dated directory. The original CSV exports and earlier SEO records are preserved. No applicant records, credentials or unrelated browser-tab data were copied into these reports.
