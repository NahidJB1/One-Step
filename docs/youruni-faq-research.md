# YourUni FAQ research for One-Step universities

Source checked: [YourUni university list](https://en.your-uni.com/university-list), 13 October 2026.

The answers below are concise paraphrases of the source pages, prepared as research rather than copy-ready website text. Dates, rankings, language scores, accommodation prices, accreditation statements and campus details should be checked against current official university sources before publication.

## Multimedia University (MMU)

Source: [MMU on YourUni](https://en.your-uni.com/university/mmu-university)

- **What is MMU's ranking?** The page reports QS subject positions of 351–400 for Electrical and Electronic Engineering and 451–500 for Computer Science. The edition is not stated in the FAQ.
- **Are MMU degrees accredited?** The page says MMU is recognised by Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education. Individual programme accreditation still needs programme-level verification.
- **When are MMU's intakes?** The FAQ lists March, July and October 2023. These dates are historical and should not be reused as current dates.
- **What English score is required?** It gives IELTS 5.0 for undergraduate study, with 5.5 or higher for some programmes, and IELTS 6.0 or higher for postgraduate study.
- **Which registration documents are requested?** A PDF of every passport page, a white-background photograph and the high-school certificate.
- **Is on-campus accommodation available?** The page says both campus housing and nearby off-campus options are available.
- **What off-campus housing is available?** It mentions condominiums, hostels and houses near MMU, with accommodation assistance offered by the agency.
- **Where is MMU located?** It lists campuses in Cyberjaya, Selangor and Bukit Beruang, Melaka.
