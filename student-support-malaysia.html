Plan my arrival

Support, step by step

These are the ways One-Step helps students after arrival. The sequence depends on your arrival date, university instructions and confirmed appointments.

  1. 01

    Airport pickup

    A welcome when you land

    We receive students at the airport and help them reach their destination. Contact us before travelling to agree your pickup, meeting point and arrival arrangements.

    Before this step: Confirm your airport, terminal, arrival time, destination and how to reach the team if your flight changes.

  2. 02

    Accommodation assistance

    Find a place to settle in

    We help you look for a rental room or accommodation that fits your needs and budget. We guide you through the arrangements so you know what to ask before committing.

    Before this step: Discuss the campus location, monthly budget, room preference, deposit, utilities and tenancy terms before paying.

  3. 03

    Medical screening support

    Help with your clinic visit

    We help coordinate the required medical screening and take students to the clinic as arranged. Your university’s international office can confirm the approved screening route and timing for your application.

    Before this step: Keep your passport and the documents requested by the clinic ready. Confirm the appointment and charges in advance.

  4. 04

    University registration

    Get your first campus steps organised

    We take students to the university and help with passport submission, enrolment and registration. We guide you through the relevant offices and the next steps requested by your institution.

    Before this step: Check the university’s document list, fee-payment requirements and appointment details. Request an acknowledgement when submitting your passport.

  5. 05

    Job-search guidance

    Explore suitable opportunities

    We help students with their job search and finding suitable opportunities. Any work must comply with your Student Pass conditions and current Malaysian rules; university and Immigration approval may be required.

    Before this step: Confirm your eligibility with the university’s international office before accepting work. Employment, income and placement are not guaranteed.

Before you arrange support

Is support free or included in my application?

Contact us for a breakdown of what is included in your arrangement and what is charged separately. Confirm transport, accommodation deposits, rent, clinic charges and university fees before making commitments.

Which airports and cities do you cover?

Tell us your arrival airport and university campus. We will confirm whether we can support your route and dates before you rely on a pickup or appointment.

How far ahead should I arrange pickup?

Contact us before finalising your arrival arrangements. Agree the booking lead time, meeting point, contact person and plan for flight changes directly with the team.

Will someone accompany me?

We offer airport pickup and accompany students to clinic and university visits as arranged. Confirm the date, transport and accompanying contact for your specific appointment.

Can I start working as soon as I arrive?

Do not assume so. Your eligibility depends on your immigration status, permitted work and any required approval. Confirm the current rules with your university’s international office and the Immigration Department before accepting a job. Our support does not guarantee a job or permission to work.

Official guidance for your next steps

For medical screening and work eligibility, consult the current official guidance and your university. Service descriptions above reflect One-Step’s support; regulatory decisions remain with the relevant authorities.
