Who these terms cover

These terms describe the use of the OneStepMy website, presented under the One-Step name, and the student guidance and processing services described here. OneStepMy has helped international students explore study in Malaysia since 2024. Our office and contact details are listed in the footer and on our Contact page. We do not require students to sign a separate service agreement.

Free resources and counselling

The resources, data and guides on this website are free to access. We do not charge for counselling. Free website access and counselling do not mean that university fees, third-party costs or an institution-specific OneStepMy processing fee are included.

University commissions and processing fees

We receive recruitment commissions from some universities. If a student chooses an institution that requires additional processing support and has no policy of paying recruitment commissions, a separate OneStepMy processing fee may apply. The amount is explained for the chosen institution; contact our team to confirm it before proceeding.

For this fee, processing is complete only when the student receives the visa. The fee is payable to OneStepMy at that point. It is not payable merely because an application has been opened or submitted.

Once processing is complete and the fee is paid, the OneStepMy processing fee is non-refundable because it covers work already completed. A later cancellation or change of study plans does not reverse that completed work. This policy does not limit any refund or remedy required by applicable law. University and EMGS charges have their own refund rules, described below.

University payments and payment assistance

University fees are payable to the respective university. Where a student uses our payment assistance, our team may receive the money, pay the university on the student’s behalf and provide receipts.

Before transferring money through this arrangement, confirm the recipient, amount, purpose and payment instructions directly with OneStepMy using the contact details on this website. Keep the payment confirmation and receipts. Payment assistance does not change which university fee or payment deadline applies.

University cancellations and refunds

We help students request refunds from their institution under that institution’s applicable cancellation and refund policy. Eligibility, deductions, required documents and payment timing depend on the institution and the stage of the application, subject to mandatory legal rights. We cannot promise a refund or a particular refund amount.

EMGS-related charges must be considered separately. Different components and application stages have different refund conditions under the official EMGS refund policy. Some processing charges become non-refundable after the relevant stage; other unused components may remain eligible for a refund. Do not assume that every EMGS-related charge is refundable or that all components follow one rule.

Where payment or the refund request is handled through a university, confirm its payment breakdown and its refund procedures as well as the EMGS rules for the underlying components. Ask which amounts are university charges, EMGS charges and any separate institutional charges. A university’s refund does not automatically refund a completed OneStepMy processing fee.

Your application and responsibilities

Provide accurate information and genuine, complete documents. Check names, course choices, campus, intake and the details in your application and offer before confirming them. Follow the university’s instructions and deadlines, and tell us promptly if your plans or circumstances change.

Our support helps you navigate the process. Admission, scholarship, immigration, medical and employment decisions are made by the relevant institutions, authorities or providers. We cannot guarantee these outcomes.

Students under 18

Students under 18 must contact OneStepMy through a parent or guardian and cannot open an application file independently. A parent or guardian must remain involved throughout the application and processing. Contact our team together to confirm the documents, consent and institution-specific requirements.

Documents, conversations and privacy

We treat student documents, personal information and private conversations as confidential. We use application information for the processing and student support requested. Processing an application may require documents to be submitted to the chosen institution and relevant processing bodies; contact us about the required recipients before submitting sensitive documents.

We do not publish private conversations or use student documents, photographs or success stories for marketing without the student’s explicit permission. Agreeing to application processing is not permission to use a student’s identity or story in publicity. Contact us with any question or concern about your information.

Support in Malaysia

Airport pickup, accommodation assistance, clinic visits and university registration support depend on confirmed arrangements, availability and institutional requirements. See Student support in Malaysia and confirm the service, timing, coverage and any third-party charges with our team. Job-search guidance does not guarantee employment or permission to work.

Using website information

We aim to provide accurate information and transparent guidance. Courses, fees, scholarships, rankings and requirements can change. Confirm the current conditions with the issuing university or authority before deciding or paying. A page update date does not establish that every underlying fee or requirement was reviewed on that date.

If you find an error, contact us so we can check it. Automated access preferences are published in robots.txt; the public agent resources do not submit applications or provide access to student accounts.

Questions, concerns and applicable law

Contact OneStepMy at info@onestepmy.com or through the phone and office details in the footer if you have a question, correction or complaint. Explain the issue and include relevant application references or payment receipts where appropriate.

Our services are subject to the applicable laws of Bangladesh and Malaysia, as relevant to the student, transaction and service. These terms do not exclude or limit rights or remedies that applicable law does not allow to be excluded or limited.
