Q27: I want to know about the post study work visa or graduate pass in malaysia after I finish my studies.
Status: answered
A27: The Graduate Pass is a **12-month Social Visit Pass** for eligible international graduates who complete a Bachelor's degree or higher in Malaysia. It permits part-time work in approved sectors without employer sponsorship; it does not promise unrestricted full-time employment. Apply while the Student Pass is still valid, with a passport valid for at least 18 months, completion evidence, 12-month local health insurance, a Malaysian sponsor earning at least RM1,500/month, and the personal bond. Nationality restrictions apply. Tell me your nationality and current pass status to check the supplied eligibility rules.
